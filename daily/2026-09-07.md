We study entire graphical translating solutions of the mean curvature flow,

 Every such graph is mean&#45;convex, since its mean curvature is the vertical component of its unit normal. In dimension two, mean&#45;convex translating solitons are convex, and an entire graphical translator is therefore the rotationally symmetric bowl soliton. In higher dimensions Wang constructed non&#45;rotational entire convex translating graphs. A detailed analysis of this layer, together with weighted Jacobi theory on the BDG graph and global barriers, yields the desired entire solutions.
