**原始英文摘要**

Let $u>0$ be the first Dirichlet $p$&#45;eigenfunction on a bounded convex domain $Ω\subset\mathbb R^N$, and set 

$$X_Ω:=|\nabla\log u|^{p-2}\nabla\log u.$$

 We study whether the sharp one&#45;dimensional two&#45;point modulus for $X_Ω$, which for $p=2$ reduces to the logarithmic&#45;gradient estimate of Andrews and Clutterbuck, persists for $p\neq2$. We prove sharp estimates on intervals and balls for every $p>1$, with the radial modulus on balls strictly larger than the one&#45;dimensional one. In dimensions $N\ge2$, however, the one&#45;dimensional modulus fails on general convex domains for every $p\neq2$. For $1<p<2$, at every sufficiently small fixed scale there are smooth uniformly convex domains for which the corresponding two&#45;point flux tends to zero. For $p>2$, on thin domains $Ω_\varepsilon=D\times(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$, with $D\subset\mathbb R^{N-1}$ bounded and convex, the normalized first eigenfunctions satisfy 

$$u_\varepsilon(x,\varepsilon z)\longrightarrow \frac{φ(z)}{φ(0)} \left(\frac{G_D(x)}{G_D(x_0)}\right)^{2/p}$$

 locally uniformly in $D\times(-1,1)$, where $φ$ and $G_D$ are the first Dirichlet eigenfunctions of the $p$&#45;Laplacian on $(-1,1)$ and of the Laplacian on $D$, respectively. This yields the failure for $p>2$. Finally, for arbitrary $C^2$ functions, positivity of the symmetric differential of the $p$&#45;gradient implies convexity, and the converse holds universally if and only if $p=2$.
