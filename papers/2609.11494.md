**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Theorems 2.1–2.4 分别确定质量次临界、超临界、临界及 $\beta \le 0$ 的极小能级。
   - **路线：** 用加权 CKN 不等式将耦合项控制在两个加权质量上，按 p&#43;q 与质量临界指数比较能量缩放$；\beta \le 0$ 时缩放方向使极小能级不可达$，\beta >0$ 时则保留正耦合收益。
2. **主张：** Lemmas 3.1–3.7 通过 fibering map 和 Palais–Smale 序列构造正基态。
   - **路线：** 沿 $(u,v)\mapsto (t^{a}u,t^{b}v)$ 计算 $\Phi _\beta (t)$，其两个临界点的符号区分稳定分支；Lemma 3.5 把极大值变分化，Lemma 3.6–3.7 给出满足质量约束的 Palais–Smale 序列与非消失。
3. **主张：** Proposition 3.1 和后续阈值比较确定 $\beta _{0}$ 以及质量单调性。
   - **路线：** 对质量参数作缩放比较，得到 $m_\beta (\gamma _{1},\gamma _{2})\le m_\beta (\rho _{1},\rho _{2})$ 的单调关系；结合 fibering 临界点存在区间，定义 $\beta _{0}$ 并排除 $0<\beta <\beta _{0}$ 的基态。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we study the coupled singular weighted elliptic system $$\left\{ \begin{aligned} -\operatorname{div}(|x|^{-2a}\nabla u)+\lambda_1 \frac{u}{|x|^{2a}}&=\beta p\frac{|v|^q|u|^{p-2}u}{|x|^{b(p+q)}}+\frac{|u|^{2^{\sharp}-2}u}{|x|^{b2^{\sharp}}},\quad\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^N, -\operatorname{div}(|x|^{-2a}\nabla v)+\lambda_2\frac{v}{|x|^{2a}}&=\beta q\frac{|u|^p|v|^{q-2}v}{|x|^{b(p+q)}}+\frac{|v|^{2^\sharp-2}v}{|x|^{b{2^\sharp}}},\quad\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^N, \displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^N}\frac{|u|^2}{|x|^{2a}} dx=\rho_1^2,\quad &\int_{\mathbb{R}^N}\frac{|v|^2}{|x|^{2a}}dx=\rho_2^2. \end{aligned} \right. $$ where $N\ge3$, $\beta\in\mathbb{R}$, $\rho_1,\rho_2>0$, $\max\{0,\tfrac{N-4}{2}\}\le a<\tfrac{N-2}{2}$, $a<b<a+1$, $2^{\sharp}:=\frac{2N}{N-2(1+a-b)}$, $p,q>1$ and $2<p+q<2^{\sharp}$. Here $2^\sharp$ is the critical exponent of the Caffarelli&#45;&#45;Kohn&#45;&#45;Nirenberg inequality, and $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in\mathbb{R}$ are unknown, since the two weighted masses are prescribed. We show that the ground state level is not attained for $\beta\le0$. For $\beta>0$ we obtain positive normalized ground states, with positive Lagrange multipliers, in the mass subcritical, mass critical, and mass supercritical regimes, for explicit ranges of $\beta$. In the mass supercritical regime, a threshold $\beta_0\ge0$ appears: a ground state exists for every $\beta>\beta_0$ and does not exist for $0<\beta<\beta_0$. We give conditions on $p,q$ under which $\beta_0=0$, and we prove that $\beta_0>0$ when $p,q\ge2$. Finally, we describe the ground states as $\beta\to0^+$ and as $\beta\to+\infty$: after a dilation, they converge to the ground states of the limit system without critical terms, or one component vanishes and the other concentrates on an extremal of the Caffarelli&#45;&#45;Kohn&#45;&#45;Nirenberg inequality.
