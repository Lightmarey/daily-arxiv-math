**完成的工作**

本文研究压力驱动的异质细胞群模型中严格分离的一维行波和不可压圆形波。作者把两类细胞的反应–交叉扩散系统化为自由边界压力问题，在行波坐标中用单调剖面和 $w(u)=-u'$ 将波速问题化为带移动边界的广义 porous–Fisher 方程；Benguria–Depassier 型试验函数给出一般压力律和增长率下的变分波速上下界。随后在 $\gamma \to \infty $ 的不可压极限中形式求出显式波速和压力/界面速度，并线性化圆形界面，按角向 Fourier 模式计算扰动增长率。数值实验比较波速界和指状不稳定性。

**使用技术**

- 自由边界压力重写
- 单调行波剖面与相平面变量
- Benguria–Depassier 变分波速界
- 不可压极限的形式渐近
- 圆形界面角向 Fourier 扰动分析

**可能的突破**

论文把异质细胞的严格分离行波波速写成适用于一般压力律和增长动力学的近显式变分界，并在不可压极限得到显式表征；圆形波的角向增长率进一步显示二维稳定性不只由两种迁移率大小决定，可出现指状失稳。

**限制与不确定性**

文章主要给出正式行波构造、变分计算、线性化和数值比较，正文没有定理/引理式的完整证明结构；圆形波稳定性是近径向线性分析，一维稳定性论证也被作者说明是 formal，高维非线性稳定性和失稳后的非线性演化尚未建立。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 一维 segregated travelling wave 的线性化算子由界面 profile 的导数确定。
   - **路线：** 对 travelling profile 设 $u=U(\xi )+\epsilon e^(\lambda t)\phi (\xi )$，在两侧相区分别线性化 reaction 与 diffusion，并以界面连续和通量条件拼接 eigenmode。
2. **主张：** 一维波的 stability 判据由谱实部和 Evans 型 matching 决定。
   - **路线：** 分别求两侧线性 ODE 的 decaying modes，界面条件形成 determinant；所有零点满足 Re $\lambda <0$ 时扰动衰减，存在正根则出现 segregation instability。
3. **主张：** 圆形 segregated wave 的径向扰动按 Fourier mode 分解。
   - **路线：** 令界面 $r=R+\epsilon e^(\lambda t)e^(im\theta )$，把 curvature、pressure 和 cell&#45;density perturbation 按 m 模态分离；每个 m 由独立的 radial Bessel matching system 决定。
4. **主张：** 数值 dispersion relation 区分稳定、长波和高阶模式。
   - **路线：** 对每个 m 求 interface determinant 的 $\lambda $ 根，比较其最大实部随 radius、growth rate 和 pressure 参数的变化$；m=0$ 是径向模态$，m\ge 1$ 描述形状破缺。

**原始英文摘要**

We consider a minimal pressure&#45;based model of heterogeneous cell populations consisting of proliferative and non&#45;proliferative cells with different mobilities. The model is formulated as a system of reaction&#45;&#45;cross&#45;&#45;diffusion equations describing the spatio&#45;temporal dynamics of the cell densities. The model is known to admit one&#45;dimensional travelling wave solutions with strictly segregated components: non&#45;proliferative cells occupy a finite region at the leading edge, while proliferative cells remain at the rear. However, the speed, parameter dependence, and stability of these waves remain poorly understood. In this work, we derive an almost explicit variational bound on the wave speed by reformulating the problem as a free&#45;boundary problem for a generalised porous&#45;&#45;Fisher equation. The estimates we obtain apply to general pressure laws and growth kinetics, agree closely with the results of numerical simulations, and become sharp in the incompressible limit, where we formally recover a fully explicit characterisation of the wave speed. We then analyse the stability of the waves to show that segregated waves are stable only when non&#45;proliferative cells are more mobile than proliferative cells. Finally, motivated by numerical observations of finger&#45;like protrusions, we investigate the stability of incompressible segregated circular waves through asymptotic shape&#45;perturbation analysis. This yields explicit expressions for the pressure, interface velocity, and growth rates of angular modes, thereby making evident the destabilisation mechanisms that may lead to the emergence of fingering instability. Interestingly, we find that, in contrast with the one&#45;dimensional case, the stability of such circular waves is not determined solely by the relative value of the mobility coefficients, and thus instabilities may arise irrespective of which cell type has the larger mobility.
