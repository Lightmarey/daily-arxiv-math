Theorems 1–3 从 Neumann 色散估计推进到能量临界波方程的全局适定性和散射。

**限制与不确定性**

域几何、Neumann 边界及能量临界非线性是必要框架；色散估计按切向/法向频区分段。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Theorem 1 给出全局和局部 Neumann 色散界。
   - **路线：** 用 Green function 与本征模表示，按频率和切向方向分区并控制法向通量。
2. **主张：** Theorems 4–6 建立主要半经典频区的局部估计。
   - **路线：** 对 $a\le h^{3(1-\epsilon )}$ 与 $a\ge h^{3(1-\epsilon )}$ 分别使用 Airy–Poisson 求和、驻相和几何基数估计。
3. **主张：** Theorem 3 由色散界和 Strichartz 理论推出临界全局适定性与散射。
   - **路线：** 先从 Theorem 1/2 获得 admissible Strichartz 对，再用能量守恒、局部理论和小数据/迭代论证完成全局延拓。

**原始英文摘要**

We establish local&#45;in&#45;time dispersive and sharp Strichartz estimates for the linear wave equation with Neumann boundary conditions inside a cylindrical convex domain $Ω\subset \mathbb{R}^3$ featuring a non&#45;empty smooth boundary $\partialΩ$. The cylindrical geometry dictates that the nonnegative radius of curvature vanishes along the axial direction. By utilizing an explicit spectral analysis based on the zeros of the Airy function derivative, alongside a tailored Airy&#45;&#45;Poisson summation formula, we capture the microlocal behavior of multi&#45;reflected waves and caustics. Finally, we apply these sharp Strichartz estimates without loss of derivatives to prove local well&#45;posedness and small&#45;data global well&#45;posedness for the energy&#45;critical quintic nonlinear wave equation &#40;NLW&#41; subject to Neumann boundary conditions.
