**完成的工作**

论文研究 HPW 缺陷趋于零的带标点流形序列是否收敛到相应模型空间。负曲率 Cartan–Hadamard 情形，作者用径向比较和缺陷分解得到双曲空间的 pointed Gromov–Hausdorff 极限；非负 Ricci 情形则通过矩项归一化、对称递减重排和体积比较得到 Euclidean 极限及尺度因子。最后在 Gaussian profile 上把 HPW 缺陷定量控制为模型距离。

**使用技术**

- HPW 缺陷分解
- 径向 Hessian/Laplacian 比较
- Gromov–Hausdorff 紧性
- Bishop–Gromov 与 Pólya–Szegő

**可能的突破**

论文把函数不确定性缺陷的消失转译为整体流形几何的模型刚性，并分别识别负曲率的双曲极限和非负 Ricci 的带矩尺度 Euclidean 极限，同时给出 Gaussian 测试剖面的定量距离控制。

**限制与不确定性**

几何收敛需要曲率夹逼、Ricci 非负或相应体积条件及归一化矩界；定量刚性只针对选定 Gaussian profiles 和定义的距离量，不覆盖无曲率下界、非完备流形或任意近极值函数。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Theorem 1.1 将负曲率流形上的 HPW 缺陷拆为梯度项、矩项和径向比较误差，并推出任意固定半径球趋于 $Hn_c$。
   - **路线：** 沿基点极坐标使用 Hessian/Laplacian 比较，把 HPW 等式中的径向积分与模型函数逐项比较；缺陷趋零迫使体积比和径向度量系数达到双曲模型值，再以曲率下界和体积上界触发 Cheeger–Gromov 紧性。
2. **主张：** Theorem 1.2 在 Gaussian profiles 上建立 Cartan–Hadamard 的定量刚性，将 HPW 缺陷控制为指向 $Hn_c$ 的距离。
   - **路线：** 对模型 Gaussian 计算缺陷随半径的显式表达，利用径向体积密度偏离模型的单调性和积分表示，将每个半径的几何偏差下界积累到总缺陷；反向取平方根得到 Gromov–Hausdorff 或体积距离界。
3. **主张：** Theorem 1.3 证明非负 Ricci 流形序列在适当矩归一化下收敛到 Euclidean 空间，并确定尺度因子由 moment term 给出。
   - **路线：** Bishop–Gromov 比较先给出体积比单调和非塌缩，Pólya–Szegő 将 HPW 缺陷与对称递减函数的欧氏能量联系；缺陷消失使重排剖面收敛，再用 Colding 紧性和 Dini 收敛把体积比提升为 Euclidean 度量极限。
4. **主张：** Theorem 1.4 和 Corollary 1.1 给出 $Ric\ge 0$ 情形的曲率修正 HPW 不等式及非欧流形的严格次优性。
   - **路线：** 利用径向体积密度的分布式 divergence 恒等式重写修正项，证明它非负并在等式时强制所有球的体积比达到欧氏值；若流形非欧，则某个半径存在严格亏损，Gaussian 测试使原 AVR 形式无法达到最优。

**原始英文摘要**

The classical Heisenberg Pauli Weyl &#40;HPW&#41; inequality exhibits a strong rigidity phenomenon on Riemannian manifolds i.e. on Cartan Hadamard manifolds and those with non&#45;negative Ricci curvature, the validity of the Euclidean HPW inequality or the existence of extremizers strictly forces the manifold to be isometric to Euclidean space, $\mathbb{R}^n$. This geometric discrepancy motivates the study of curvature dependent corrections and their associated stability properties. In this article, we investigate the geometric stability of the HPW inequality. Specifically, given a sequence of pointed Riemannian manifolds and appropriately normalized functions with a vanishing HPW deficit, we address whether the sequence converges to the corresponding model space in the pointed Gromov Hausdorff topology. We prove that for pinched Cartan Hadamard manifolds with sectional curvature bounded above by $c < 0$, the manifolds converge to the model hyperbolic space $\mathbb{H}^n_c$. In the non&#45;negative Ricci curvature setting, we establish convergence to $\mathbb{R}^n$, up to a metric rescaling factor governed by the sequence's moment term. Consequently, we deduce that the known HPW inequality formulated via the asymptotic volume ratio &#40;AVR&#41; is suboptimal for non negatively Ricci curved manifolds not isometric to Euclidean space. To resolve this, we introduce a curvature corrected HPW inequality for this setting, analogous to the Cartan Hadamard case. Finally, we establish quantitative rigidity estimates in both curvature regimes, demonstrating that the HPW deficit when evaluated at Gaussian profiles which explicitly controls an appropriately defined distance to the respective model space.
