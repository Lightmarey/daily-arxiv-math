**可能的突破**

论文发现并严格实现了把顶阶电场功直接并入加权能量时间导数的补偿机制，使 Euler–Poisson 的准中性稳定性在高阶 Sobolev 范数中不再损失 $\\epsilon ^\{-1\}$，从而支持任意阶展开和固定时间一致误差。

**限制与不确定性**

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Proposition 5\.1、Theorem 5\.1 和 Proposition 5\.5 构造平滑膨胀简单波及有限阶 Debye 复合剖面。
   - **路线：** 先求有效 Euler 系统的单调 Burgers 型简单波，再逐阶解 Poisson–Debye 约束得到密度和势修正；用有限层级的余项方程和 cutoff 兼容，把各阶剖面拼成在 $t\\in \[t0,T\]$ 上光滑且残差可控的复合解。
2. **主张：** Lemma 5\.7 和 Proposition 5\.8 建立非线性 Poisson 约束的统一 screened inverse 与兼容初始势。
   - **路线：** 对密度保持正下界的椭圆 Poisson 方程作线性化，利用屏蔽算子的 coercivity 取得 $H^s$ 到势/梯度的统一逆估计；隐函数/压缩映射再给出小扰动下的非线性求解器和准备初值。
3. **主张：** Lemma 5\.12–5\.15 与 Proposition 5\.16 闭合解与复合剖面之差的高阶稳定估计。
   - **路线：** 对差分系统逐空间多重指标交换微分，低阶使用电场零阶抵消；顶阶把时间微分的 Poisson 约束乘到加权电场能量中，电功项与密度时间导数精确配对，从而吸收 $1/\\epsilon $ 项，再用 Moser/交换子估计闭合 bootstrap。
4. **主张：** Theorem 4\.1 和 Corollary 4\.2 得到固定时间区间的统一稳定性与任意阶准中性展开。
   - **路线：** 以 Proposition 5\.17 的固定 $\\epsilon $ 局部理论作为延拓入口，在 bootstrap 区间上应用 Proposition 5\.16 保持初始裕量；最大延拓时间若有限则高阶界允许继续解，故覆盖 \[t0,T\]，再用兼容 Debye 复合剖面的余项估计逐阶识别 $\\epsilon $ 展开。

**原始英文摘要**

We study the warm-ion Euler--Poisson system with Maxwell--Boltzmann electrons on the cylinder $\\R\\times\\T$ in the quasineutral regime\. Taking a smooth expanding planar simple wave of the effective Euler system as the reference state, we construct an even Debye expansion through arbitrary finite order $M$ with a residual of $O\(\\eps^\{2M\+2\}\)$\. We rigorously establish nonlinear stability on every fixed interval $\[t\_0,T\]$ \($t\_0\\ge0$\), achieving a lifespan and energy constants strictly independent of the Debye length $0&lt;\\eps\\le\\eps\_0$\. To overcome the singular scaling of the electric field, we develop a novel compensated energy topology that couples the warm-ion symmetrizer to the time-differentiated nonlinear Poisson constraint\. By integrating the top-order electric work directly into the time derivative of a weighted energy functional, this mechanism controls the potential in $H^s$ and its gradient in $\\eps H^s$, completely eliminating the $\\eps^\{-1\}$ loss typically encountered in the momentum equation\. This framework successfully governs distinct neutral end states, captures genuinely two-dimensional rotational perturbations, and yields an arbitrary-order quasineutral asymptotic expansion for prepared data, providing a critical analytical foundation for the geometric theory of multidimensional quasineutral rarefactions\.
