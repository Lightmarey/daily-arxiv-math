**原始英文摘要**

We study the time&#45;dependent Hartree&#45;&#45;Fock equation in $\R^3$ with physical Coulomb interactions in both direct and exchange channels, with exchange coupling $η$, linearized about homogeneous equilibria $γ_f=g(-i\nabla)$ having compact momentum support. Nguyen and You showed that, without exchange, two purely imaginary plasmon poles $\pm iτ_0(|k|)$ exist below a survival threshold $κ_0>0$. Existing nonlinear Hartree&#45;&#45;Fock theory requires a short&#45;range direct interaction and a smooth small exchange kernel, excluding the present Coulomb setting. On every compact band $0<k_-\le|k|\le k_+<κ_0$, we prove unconditionally for Coulomb exchange that, for small $|η|$, the poles persist, remain simple and purely imaginary, and depend real&#45;analytically on $η$. Their first&#45;order shift $τ_X$ splits into explicit static self&#45;energy and dynamic vertex corrections. Each pole yields an exact undamped mode with a closed&#45;form unit&#45;density eigenfunction, embedded in the fiber generator's essential spectrum. The causal density Green function splits into an explicit undamped sine wave and a remainder whose Laplace transform is holomorphic near the poles, while the plasmon channel satisfies uniform $t^{-3/2}$ dispersive decay and endpoint Strichartz estimates. A first&#45;order Ward&#45;type cancellation gives $|τ_X(r)|\le Cr^2$ down to $r=0$; hence exchange leaves the plasma gap unchanged to first order, and we obtain a closed formula for the stiffness correction. Numerics for a $C^9$ smoothed Fermi ball support the analysis: self&#45;energy and vertex terms cancel within $1.2\%$ on the computed range, and exchange softens the dispersion most strongly near $0.7\,κ_0$.
