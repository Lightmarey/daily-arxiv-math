**完成的工作**

本文把随机复制起点和复制叉推进写成一维群体平均动力学。作者先从带长度、位置和时间变量的未复制区间密度出发，通过左右复制叉的输运和起点/合并项把尺寸变量积分消去，得到未复制比例、叉密度与给定起点率 I\(x,t\)、叉速 v\(x,t\) 的耦合系统。正文先用固定点证明局部温和解唯一且连续依赖初值，再把叉密度除以未复制比例，沿特征线化为线性输运，从而延拓到任意有限时间。最后用反向因果锥上的起点质量 $m\_I$ 给出最坏位置的未复制率和近完成时间 $T\\epsilon $ 上界，并将该界用于期望复制时间和 Repli-seq 型数值比较。

**可能的突破**

关键新结论是把“最后未复制区”从全局起点总量改写为局部反向因果锥内的起点质量：该几何量直接控制任意位置的未复制率，并给出几乎完成时间的显式上界；归一化输运还把局部解的延拓条件转化为可验证的正性与有界性条件。

**限制与不确定性**

模型是一维有效基因组上的群体平均近似，起点率 I 和叉速 v 被作为外部给定场，且全局定理要求初值满足未复制比例的正下界、系数有界/正则及文中兼容条件$\(2\.35\)、\(3\.3\)。T\\epsilon $ 与期望时间是这些假设下的上界或积分估计，不是单细胞随机完成时间的精确分布；数值 Repli-seq 比较也不替代动力学假设。

**原始英文摘要**

DNA replication requires the coordination of origin firing and fork progression to ensure the entire genome is timely duplicated before cell division\. Yet origin firing is stochastic, giving rise to the classical random completion problem of how probabilistic local events can nevertheless ensure reliable genome duplication\. Although several biological mechanisms have been proposed to resolve this problem, a quantitative account of how heterogeneous initiation and fork speed govern the persistence of the final unreplicated regions is still lacking\. To address this gap, we introduce a population-level kinetic framework that extends KJMA nucleation-and-growth models by tracking unreplicated intervals over size, genomic position and time\. We establish well-posedness of the resulting mean-field system and global existence for compatible data\. Notably, by introducing a local initiation mass function, we quantify how the density and spatial organisation of origin firing constrain replication completion, yielding novel and sharp upper bounds on both the worst-locus unreplicated fraction and locuswise near-completion time\. These results provide a rigorous and computable foundation for mapping vulnerabilities in replication completion and relating persistent unreplicated regions to replication stress and genome instability\.
