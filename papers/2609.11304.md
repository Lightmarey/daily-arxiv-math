**可能的突破**

Theorem 2\.1--2\.7 将初始 effective velocity 在边界的符号直接对应到两种结局：非负迹给出全局正密度和全局解，负迹在有限时间形成边界真空。该判据允许任意大的初始数据，关键在 BD 结构消去密度梯度坏项。

**限制与不确定性**

全局结论依赖球对称、BD 黏性系数和参数范围；负迹结论是边界真空形成而非对所有奇异类型给出统一 blow-up 率，不覆盖非径向三维流。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 质量坐标和 effective velocity 将密度演化转成一维最大值问题。
   - **路线：** 第 3\.1--3\.2 节以质量变量重写连续性方程，定义有效和横向应变率，使边界密度、通量和压力项由同一组标量函数表达。
2. **主张：** 非负边界迹保持正密度并给出全局延拓。
   - **路线：** 第 5 节先用 transverse strain rate 最大值原理控制速度，再对 reciprocal density 使用有效压力方程得到边界下界；continuation criterion 排除密度损失。
3. **主张：** 负边界迹在短时间内保持负通量。
   - **路线：** 第 6\.1 节短时控制 transverse strain rate，第 6\.2 节边界缩放并写出 flux equations；兼容初值和 observation collar 使负通量在缩放层持续。
4. **主张：** 有限容量与持续负通量迫使边界真空。
   - **路线：** 将 collar 中负通量积分到密度质量，若正密度持续则需超过有限可用容量；第 6\.5 节形成矛盾，第 6\.6 节把终端迹定位在边界，完成 Theorem 2\.7。

**排序理由**

给出任意大球对称初值下由 effective velocity 边界符号决定全局存在或边界真空的判据。

**原始英文摘要**

We establish a sharp criterion for global-in-time existence versus finite-time blow-up of strong solutions to the 3D spherically symmetric compressible Navier-Stokes equations on a solid ball, based solely on the initial sign of effective velocity\. The viscosity coefficients are assumed to satisfy the Bresch-Desjardins structure $\\mu=\\rho^\{\\alpha\}$, $\\lambda=\(\\alpha-1\)\\rho^\{\\alpha\}$, with the effective velocity given by $v=u\+\\alpha \\rho^\{\\alpha-2\}\\rho\_r$\. Previously, global existence results for strong solutions in higher dimensions were restricted to the case $\\alpha\\le 1$ with the endpoint $\\alpha=1$ corresponding to the viscous Saint-Venant \(shallow water\) system\. In this paper, we extend the global existence theory beyond this threshold to the supercritical regime $\\alpha&gt;1$\. Specifically, whenever the initial effective velocity is nonnegative on the boundary of the ball, we prove the global-in-time existence of strong solutions for arbitrarily large initial data\. The main difficulty lies in deriving a uniform lower bound for the density, which requires handling singular estimates at the center of the ball\. To overcome this, we exploit several novel quantities near the center and employ a refined maximum principle to establish, for the first time, the Lipschitz continuity of the velocity at the center\. Subsequently, we derive Dini-Gronwall-type inequalities for suitably constructed functions, which close the density lower bound estimate\. Conversely, we can construct a family of initial data for which the initial effective velocity is negative on the boundary, such that the corresponding strong solutions blow up in finite time, with vacuum appearing exactly on the boundary at the blow-up time\. Our results establish a clean dichotomy between global regularity and singularity formation, governed purely by the initial sign of the effective velocity\.
