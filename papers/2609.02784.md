**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 四边形可经等面积相似归一化参数化到紧致椭球参数域，且只需在该域上比较归一化特征值。
   - **路线：** 用仿射映射把每个四边形拉回参考正方形，计算度量矩阵和面积约束；Kröger 型下界排除参数域外可能达到正方形值的区域，Lemma 2\.2 给出紧性。
2. **主张：** 在正方形邻域，辅助二特征值泛函跨越特征值简并仍解析，且正方形是严格局部最大点。
   - **路线：** 用轮廓积分表示谱投影和特征值簇，得到 Lemma 4\.1 的解析性；在对称约化坐标计算 Hessian，Proposition 4\.2 认证谱簇间隔，Corollary 5\.5 用二阶差商在显式球内确认严格不等式。
3. **主张：** 每个远离正方形的参数盒上，第一 Rayleigh--Ritz 特征值严格低于正方形值。
   - **路线：** 在固定参考正方形上对五个非恒 Neumann 模式构造 Rayleigh--Ritz 矩阵，以区间积分包围其最小特征值上界；盒内所有参数共享同一认证界，正谱簇由局部步骤排除退化。
4. **主张：** 将局部球与外部盒覆盖合并，得到平方形唯一全局最大化结论。
   - **路线：** 有限自适应盒算法终止意味着外部区域全部被验证或丢弃，Theorem 6\.2 给出统一严格上界；与局部 Corollary 5\.5 拼接后，等面积约束下达到 $\\pi^2$ 只能是正方形的全等形。

**原始英文摘要**

We prove that among convex planar quadrilaterals of equal area, the square uniquely maximizes the first nonzero Laplace--Neumann eigenvalue\. This is the quadrilateral case of the Neumann analogue of the long-standing Pólya--Szegő conjecture, which asserts that the regular $n$-gon minimizes the first Laplace--Dirichlet eigenvalue among $n$-gons of equal area\. The proof uses a Rayleigh--Ritz lower bound, obtained from the span of the first five nonconstant Neumann modes of the square, on a smooth auxiliary functional whose minimization implies the original maximization\. Local maximality at the square follows from a symmetry-reduced Hessian, computed in closed form, together with a certified second-order difference-quotient test that establishes the local inequality on an explicit ball around the square\. Global maximality is then established by a certified box covering of the parameter space, certifying on each box a direct upper bound on the first Rayleigh--Ritz eigenvalue\. Both certifications operate entirely on integrals over a fixed reference square and avoid a posteriori finite-element bounds on the perturbed quadrilateral\.
