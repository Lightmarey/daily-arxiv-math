**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Propositions 3.5–3.6 对冻结的输运–更新线性系统给出正性、存在性和 L1 稳定估计。
   - **路线：** 沿每个 zone 的特征线积分内部输运方程，把 recruitment 边界值表示成状态和资源的线性泛函；用 Grönwall 控制沿线增长，再以对偶伴随问题把两个冻结解之差转成初值和系数差的 L1 界。
2. **主张：** Lemmas 3.7–3.9 构造非线性耦合映射的资源界、自映射和局部压缩性。
   - **路线：** 先用资源 ODE 的耗散和非负性限制资源轨道，再选取状态球使冻结解保持在同一球内；反馈函数的 Lipschitz 界和时间区间缩短给出压缩常数小于一。
3. **主张：** Theorems 3.10、3.14 和 3.15 分别给出局部适定、任意有限时间全局弱解及对初值/环境/收获的连续依赖。
   - **路线：** Banach 不动点先给出短时间唯一解；L1、资源和 $L\infty $ 先验界排除最大存在时间前的范数爆炸，故可逐段延拓；对两组数据应用线性稳定估计和 Grönwall，再利用紧时间分割得到连续依赖。
4. **主张：** Theorem 4.5、Theorem 5.3 和 Theorem 6.3 将自治约化后的正算子谱阈值、非平凡稳态和最优控制存在性分别闭合。
   - **路线：** Krein–Rutman 将正紧 next&#45;generation operator 的谱半径识别为 R0，连续参数改变使谱半径穿过 1；稳态用参数中值与正特征向量构造，控制问题则由状态连续依赖、紧 admissible 控制类和 Weierstrass 定理取到最大值。

**原始英文摘要**

We develop a rigorous analytical framework for a class of physiologically structured population models with two internal state variables, nonlocal ecological feedbacks, dynamic resources, inter&#45;zone transfer, and selective harvesting. The full model is a coupled nonlinear PDE&#45;&#45;ODE transport&#45;&#45;renewal system with endogenous inflow at the recruitment boundary, a setting in which transport, nonlocal dependence, and boundary renewal interact at the same level. For this full nonautonomous multi&#45;zone system, we prove finite&#45;horizon well&#45;posedness in a positive $L^{1}$&#45;based state space, including global existence on arbitrary bounded time intervals, uniqueness, nonnegativity, and continuous dependence on initial data, environmental forcing, and harvesting effort. We then isolate an autonomous single&#45;zone reduction at extinction and construct a positive compact next&#45;generation operator on the recruit space. In a further nonlinear stationary reduction, we prove that supercriticality of the basic reproduction number $\mathcal R_{0}>1$ yields existence of a nontrivial stationary state under a parametrized compact&#45;operator hypothesis encoding density&#45;dependent renewal feedback. Finally, for a finite&#45;horizon harvest objective over a compact Lipschitz&#45;regular admissible class, we establish existence of an optimal control. The results separate what can be proved for the full climate&#45;explicit system from what can be justified only after autonomous reduction, thereby clarifying the mathematical scope of threshold and control theory for structured populations.
