**完成的工作**

建立依赖相空间有界测度的广义伪微分量子化，包括 anti&#45;Wick、Weyl、经典和 Born&#45;&#45;Jordan 量子化；刻画参数为 1/2 的 Gevrey 符号类$、L\infty $ 符号的附加测度条件，并研究符号复合、Mizrahi 展开和算子&#45;&#45;符号 Banach 代数同构。

**使用技术**

- Wick 量子化与 Berezin 符号
- 相空间测度卷积
- Gevrey 1/2 符号估计
- Mizrahi 级数
- Levy&#45;&#45;Khintchine 表示

**可能的突破**

把不同量子化统一为有界相空间测度参数化的卷积框架，并精确指出 $L\infty $ 符号可用的测度条件，使 Levy 过程概率测度成为自然实例。

**限制与不确定性**

$L\infty $ 结果依赖额外测度假设，Weyl 量子化不满足某些 anti&#45;Wick 条件；复合公式只在指定 Gevrey/调制空间类内成立。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 一般量子化可表示为符号与相空间有界测度的卷积，并在 Wick 情形得到有界算子。
   - **路线：** 用相干态分解和 Berezin 符号把算子二次型写成 F 与测度卷积，利用正测度和 Schur/Plancherel 控制得到 L2 有界性。
2. **主张：** Gevrey 参数 1/2 的符号类对所有广义量子化保持，并在附加条件下包含 $L\infty $ 符号。
   - **路线：** 对高阶差分和相空间导数使用高斯权估计，测度卷积的矩界控制级数；当测度满足 Definition 2.11 的指数尾条件时，截断逼近和插值把估计延拓到有界符号。
3. **主张：** Weyl、anti&#45;Wick、Born&#45;&#45;Jordan 等量子化之间的符号关系可由显式卷积和热核变换给出。
   - **路线：** 比较各自的相干态核，应用 Fourier 变换把量子化差异写成测度卷积；对 Weyl 情形识别调制空间，利用热核正规化得到符号反演。
4. **主张：** 在指定符号类中，复合运算形成可控的 Mizrahi 展开和 Banach 代数结构。
   - **路线：** 把两个算子作用到相干态上，逐阶展开相空间平移并用 Gevrey 余项估计求和；组合卷积核得到复合符号，绝对收敛与范数次乘性给出 Banach 代数同构。

**原始英文摘要**

This article is concerned with generalizations of pseudo&#45;differential operators in $L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$, $n\geq 1$. The definition of the new calculi depends only on bounded measures on the phase space $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ and each measure gives rise to a specific calculus. Quantizations of anti&#45;Wick, Weyl, classical and Born&#45;Jordan are particular cases of the general calculi. Classes of symbols in this framework are then studied. The Gevrey class of parameter 1/2 is a class of symbol that is common to all the general calculi, that is, a class of symbols independent on the bounded measures parametrizing the quantizations. Precise additional hypotheses on the measures are necessary in the aim to consider the larger class of symbols $L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{2n})$. This result can be applied for anti&#45;Wick but not for Weyl quantization. Concerning Weyl pseudo&#45;differential calculus, we recover the standard class of Sjöstrand and Gröchening. Then, we prove that probability measures of Lévy processes on the phase space $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ with diffusion larger than 1/4 are natural examples of measures satisfying the latter additional hypotheses in order to consider $L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{2n})$ symbols. This relation is derived using the Lévy&#45;Khintchine formula. Composition laws in that general context are next investigated. In that purpose, we give a formula for the composition of two symbols in some precise class of symbols valid for all general quantizations. General calculi are relying on Wick quantization which is therefore primarily examined for some precise classes of symbols. Additional results in that context are provided, such as Mizrahi series expansions and Banach algebra isomorphisms between operators and symbol classes.
