**完成的工作**

**可能的突破**

在不要求时间周期性的情况下，把局部 RH2 通过空间周期传播到所有尺度，并推出 N&#42; $u\in L^{2}$ 类弱解唯一可解。

**限制与不确定性**

对称主定理需要时间模连续和 square&#45;Dini 横向收敛；非对称 $A\infty $ 结论有额外边界迹假设，域推广限于兼容的 Lipschitz 图域。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Theorems 1.4–1.7 将局部 RH2、Poisson 核和 $L^{2}$ Dirichlet 可解性互相等价。
   - **路线：** 先用正解的边界 Harnack、doubling 和 Green 函数估计建立 RH2；再把 Poisson 核的 RH2 转成非切向最大函数控制，最后用层势表示得到边值数据的 $L^{2}$ 解及唯一性。
2. **主张：** Lemmas 2.18、2.23、2.31 在小于周期尺度建立边界比较和反向 Hölder。
   - **路线：** 用时间定向的抛物立方体和正解 Harnack 链比较 Green 函数，Lemma 2.31 把 RH2 与 Dirichlet 可解性转换；系数的横向 square&#45;Dini 模保证尺度迭代中的误差可求和。
3. **主张：** 大尺度伴随高度坐标 $\Phi *=\lambda +O(1)$ 传递 Green 函数衰减。
   - **路线：** 构造 $H*\Phi *=0$ 的正伴随函数并用周期性归一化其线性增长，边界比较把 Green 函数与 $\Phi *$ 的比值夹住；周期平移后迭代该比较，获得任意大尺度的 doubling 和 RH2。
4. **主张：** Theorem 1.6 将全局 RH2 变成 $L^{2}$ Dirichlet 问题的存在唯一性。
   - **路线：** 全尺度 Poisson 核估计给出 N&#42; u 的 $L^{2}$ 界，截断边界数据的解由弱紧性收敛；若边界数据为零，能量和非切向控制使解为零，完成唯一性。

**原始英文摘要**

We prove global reverse H&#92;"older estimates in $\mathrm L^2$ for the Poisson kernel of scalar divergence&#45;form parabolic operators in the upper half&#45;space. The coefficient matrix is real, symmetric, and periodic in every spatial variable $X=(\lambda,x)$, while no periodicity in time is assumed. If its boundary trace $A^0=A^0(x,t)$ has a quantitative uniform modulus of continuity in time and its convergence to $A^0$ in the transverse variable is controlled by a square&#45;Dini modulus, then the Poisson kernel satisfies a uniform $\mathrm{RH}_2$ estimate at scales below the period. Full spatial periodicity is then used to propagate this estimate to every scale. The main new ingredient in the large&#45;scale argument is a positive adjoint height coordinate $\Phi^*=\lambda+O(1)$. Boundary comparison with $\Phi^*$ yields the required large&#45;scale decay of the Green function and hence the global $\mathrm{RH}_2$ estimate. Consequently, the $\mathrm L^2$ Dirichlet problem is uniquely solvable among weak solutions $u$ satisfying $N_\ast u\in\mathrm L^2$. We also establish, for fully spatially periodic coefficients, a nonsymmetric $A_\infty$ theorem under square&#45;Dini convergence to a transverse&#45;independent boundary trace, with no temporal regularity beyond measurability. We explain how the results transfer to time&#45;independent Lipschitz graph domains under the stated coefficient and periodicity&#45;compatibility hypotheses, and we derive scale&#45;uniform boundary estimates for the periodic homogenization families considered below. The condition $D_t^{1/2}A^0\in\mathrm L^\infty_x(\mathrm{BMO}_t)$ provides a natural sufficient criterion for the temporal continuity required in the symmetric theorem.
