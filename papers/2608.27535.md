**可能的突破**

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Lemma 3.1 将拼接域的特征函数分解为偶/奇分支，并对应半域上的 Neumann/Dirichlet 谱。
   - **路线：** 镜像反射与 Dirichlet Laplacian 对易，因而特征空间按反射奇偶分解；限制到半域后，偶函数满足 Neumann 界面条件、奇函数满足 Dirichlet 界面条件，给出两列可比较的特征值。
2. **主张：** Propositions 4.2–4.3 证明细颈极限下特征值和内部特征函数收敛到基域数据。
   - **路线：** 细颈的 Poincare/质量估计使颈部 $L^{2}$ 质量消失；min–max 原理给出特征值的上下界收敛，a2 的简单性再配合椭圆内部估计得到远离颈部的 $C^{1}$ 特征函数收敛。
3. **主张：** Theorem 5.1 与 Proposition 5.2 确立严格偶奇谱分离并识别 $\lambda _{3}=\nu _{2}$ 且简单。
   - **路线：** min–max 先给 $\nu j\le \delta j$；若出现等号，则界面上同时满足 Dirichlet 与 Neumann Cauchy 数据，唯一延拓迫使特征函数为零，故严格分离。结合基域极限的谱隙，定位第三特征值为偶分支 $\nu _{2}$ 并继承简单性。
4. **主张：** Theorem 6.1 在多边形域上确定零集恰为两条简单闭曲线。
   - **路线：** 构造的严格符号保证正、负节点域至少达到指定数量；Courant 定理把总节点域数限制为三，符号变化又把零集限制在内部。把零集视为平面图并应用 Euler 关系，闭合分支数只能是两条且所有顶点度为二。
5. **主张：** Lemmas 7.1–7.2 与 Theorem 1.2 将多边形结论传到 $C\infty $ 域。
   - **路线：** 取包含关键紧集的光滑内逼近域，特征值随域收敛且特征函数在紧集上 $C^{1}$ 收敛；因此原有谱隙、简单性和符号裕量不变，再重复 Courant/Euler 节点论证得到最终反例。

**原始英文摘要**

A Counterexample to the Closed Nodal&#45;Line Conjecture for the Third Eigenfunction of the Planar Dirichlet Laplacian Zikang Deng Levitin and Yagudin conjectured in Conjecture 4.3 of LMS J. Comput. Math. 6 &#40;2003&#41; that, if the third Dirichlet eigenvalue of a connected planar domain is simple, then not all nodal lines of a corresponding eigenfunction can be closed. We construct a counterexample: a bounded connected planar domain with smooth boundary whose third Dirichlet eigenvalue is simple and for which the entire interior nodal set of every corresponding nonzero real eigenfunction is compactly contained in the domain. In fact, the nodal set consists of exactly two disjoint real&#45;analytic simple closed curves. Starting from the Payne&#45;conjecture counterexample of Dahne, Gomez&#45;Serrano, and Hou, we connect two reflected copies by a symmetric thin neck. Reflection splits the spectrum into mixed Dirichlet&#45;Neumann and Dirichlet spectra on a half&#45;domain. A transverse Poincare inequality and the min&#45;max principle yield convergence of both spectra to the base&#45;domain spectrum, while boundary unique continuation gives strict separation at equal indices and places the even second branch at the third spectral position. Local eigenfunction convergence preserves strict sign inequalities; Courant's theorem confines the nodal set; and an Euler count determines its topology. A smooth inner exhaustion completes the construction.
