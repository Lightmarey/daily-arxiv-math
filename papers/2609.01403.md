**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Theorem 1.1 给出混合算子基本解在原点附近的主导幂次和修正项。
   - **路线：** 对 Fourier 乘子 $m(\xi )=(|\xi |^{2}+|\xi |^{2s})^{-1}$ 做高低频分解，以齐次分布反演和 cutoff 估计控制余项。
2. **主张：** Theorem 1.2 给出穿孔球内解的 Bôcher 型分解。
   - **路线：** 先用 Theorem 1.1 构造奇异基本解，再对差函数应用局部光滑性和可去奇点论证，得到奇异系数与正则余项。
3. **主张：** Theorem 1.3 和 Theorem 1.4 分别给出正最大值原理与反对称最大值原理。
   - **路线：** 加入常数势 $\Lambda -c\ge 0$，把方程转为适合比较的非负算子；对反对称情形在半球域利用交换反射和边界符号完成比较。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we study the fundamental solution of the mixed local&#45;nonlocal operator $ -Δ+(-Δ)^s,$ $ 0<s<1. $ We derive precise asymptotic expansions of the fundamental solution and its gradient, up to the first nontrivial correction term, both near the origin and at infinity. The expansions describe the transition between the local and nonlocal diffusion scales and include the critical regimes in which logarithmic terms occur. As applications of these asymptotics, we establish a distributional Bôcher&#45;type theorem for nonnegative supersolutions with an isolated singularity. We further obtain quantitative positive and antisymmetric maximum principles on punctured balls.
