**完成的工作**

构造高维 mean curvature flow 的 entire graphical translators：先以 BDG 非平面 minimal graph 为基底，建立 Jacobi 逆和加权 Schauder 估计，再通过内外层匹配和全局有序势垒得到一参数 translator 族。

**使用技术**

- BDG 图形
- Jacobi 算子
- 加权 Schauder 估计
- 内外层匹配
- 有序势垒

**可能的突破**

在 $N\ge 8$ 的高维中把非平面 BDG 图形修正为 entire translating solutions，形成 Bernstein 型非仿射族。

**限制与不确定性**

维数、对称性 $O(4)\times O(4)$ 和小参数范围严格；translator 构造依赖 BDG 输入，不涵盖任意维数或无对称图形。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** BDG 图形和其 Jacobi 场提供非平面基准及正性。
   - **路线：** 将 $O(4)\times O(4)-$不变 BDG graph 写成 $\Gamma $ 上的函数，分析端点展开和正 Jacobi 场；最大值原理给出线性化算子的基本控制。
2. **主张：** 外区的非线性误差可由加权 Jacobi 逆消除。
   - **路线：** 在大半径区域展开 translator 方程，先解 borderline linear correction，再用 weighted Schauder 估计控制二次/三次余项；快速 Jacobi inverse 给出唯一校正。
3. **主张：** 内层 profile 与外层修正可在重叠区匹配。
   - **路线：** 对缩放内变量构造 inner profile，用精确 homogeneous symbol 和 coefficient matching 调整外层参数；重叠区误差按 $\epsilon $ 次序相消。
4. **主张：** 全局有序势垒夹逼产生真正的 translator。
   - **路线：** 把 inner/outer 近似拼接为 global sub/supersolutions，利用 ordered barriers 和 Perron/极限方法取 $\epsilon \to 0$，得到 Theorem 7.3/8.1 的 entire graph。

**原始英文摘要**

We study entire graphical translating solutions of the mean curvature flow, 

$$\operatorname{div}\!\left(\frac{\nabla G}{\sqrt{1+|\nabla G|^2}}\right) =\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+|\nabla G|^2}} \qquad\text{in }\mathbb R^N.$$

 Every such graph is mean&#45;convex, since its mean curvature is the vertical component of its unit normal. In dimension two, mean&#45;convex translating solitons are convex, and an entire graphical translator is therefore the rotationally symmetric bowl soliton. In higher dimensions Wang constructed non&#45;rotational entire convex translating graphs. We prove that a further loss of rigidity occurs at the Bernstein dimension: for every $N\ge 8$ there exists a one&#45;parameter family of entire graphical translators that are mean&#45;convex but not convex. The construction starts from the Bombieri&#45;&#45;De Giorgi&#45;&#45;Giusti &#40;BDG&#41; entire minimal graph in $\mathbb R^8$ and develops a singular perturbation theory for the translator equation around it. The main new feature is a transition layer near Simons' cone: the translating term breaks the odd symmetry of the minimal graph, and after a suitable recentering the matching problem is governed by a parabolic inner equation. A detailed analysis of this layer, together with weighted Jacobi theory on the BDG graph and global barriers, yields the desired entire solutions.
