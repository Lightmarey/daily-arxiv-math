**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 混合 Dirichlet--Neumann 电势问题具有层势表示和稳定估计。
   - **路线：** 对边界分区的 Green 表示作单层/双层势分解，利用边界积分算子可逆性控制势和法向导数。
2. **主张：** 单个小 Robin 扰动的首阶效应由局部边界算子决定。
   - **路线：** 在扰动尺度上展开 Robin 算子和局部平坦化坐标，把全局背景场冻结为边界点值；匹配内外解后得到首项系数和局部极化量。
3. **主张：** 多个扰动的相互作用可由分离距离控制并展开。
   - **路线：** 将每个局部边界层的远场尾部代回其他扰动点，按 separation assumption 迭代求解耦合系数；交互项由 Green 核衰减控制。
4. **主张：** 不同边界缩放下电势可得到统一渐近展开和误差。
   - **路线：** 把 Robin 扰动改写为 Neumann 小区域或 Dirichlet 小孔的极限，使用容量/极化项和 Proposition 7\.1--7\.2 的边界积分估计控制余项。

**原始英文摘要**

Inspired by questions related to inverse problems and shape optimization, we derive an asymptotic expansion of the voltage potential, solution to a model elliptic second-order partial differential equation, under small perturbations of its boundary conditions\. More precisely, the homogeneous Dirichlet or homogeneous Neumann boundary condition in a fixed, reference configuration of the problem is replaced by a Robin boundary condition with admittance $k\_\\varepsilon &gt; 0$ on a \`\`small'' subset $ω\_\\varepsilon$ of the boundary of the ambient domain, vanishing at the limit $\\varepsilon \\to 0$\. In each of these two situations, a general asymptotic formula is established for the voltage potential, which rests on minimal assumptions about the shape of the vanishing subset $ω\_\\varepsilon$ and the parameter $k\_\\varepsilon$\. The scalings of these expansions, capturing the intensity of the perturbation, are measured by new quantities called \`\`Robin-Dirichlet'' capacity or a \`\`Robin-Neumann'' capacity, which depend on the geometry of $ω\_\\varepsilon$ and on the value of the parameter $k\_\\varepsilon$\. We analyze how these quantities compare to more classical measures of the \`\`smallness'' of $ω\_\\varepsilon$, such as the capacity or the Neumann capacity of $ω\_\\varepsilon$, according to the behavior of $k\_\\varepsilon$ as $\\varepsilon \\to 0$\.
