**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Kato 类势生成具有 Feynman--Kac 表示的 Schrödinger 半群。
   - **路线：** 用 Kato 小量性控制 Feynman--Kac 指数矩，得到半群在 $L2/L\\infty $ 与 Sobolev 空间的有界性和时间正则性。
2. **主张：** 有界轨道满足统一的 $L\\infty 、H1$ 先验估计。
   - **路线：** 对抛物化方程作能量测试并用半群表示处理势项，先控制 L2 和尾部，再以 bootstrap 得到局部正则性和解集的有界性。
3. **主张：** 有界动力学的孤立邻域具有非零拓扑指标。
   - **路线：** 在截断球内构造 isolating neighborhood，利用非共振谱分解计算线性流的 Morse 指标；Lipschitz 小扰动保持指数对。
4. **主张：** 不变集指标非平凡推出 standing wave 与 connecting orbit。
   - **路线：** 由 Conley 指标 continuation 得到不动点/平衡，排除零解后恢复原 Schrödinger 椭圆方程；对不同平衡的不变流线集应用连接轨道定理。

**原始英文摘要**

We establish the existence of standing waves for a nonlinear Schrodinger equation with potentials belonging to the Kato class and an $L^\\infty$-bounded nonlinearity, whose Lipschitz constant is smaller than the distance from zero to the essential spectrum of the linear part\. We consider both the nonresonant and resonant cases\. Our approach is based on the Conley index theory applied to study invariant sets of the associated parabolic semiflow\. Using properties of the Schrodinger semigroup with Kato-class potentials, which follow from its Feynman-Kac representation, we derive a priori estimates for bounded solutions in the $L^\\infty$ and $L^2$ norms, as well as regularity bounds in Sobolev spaces\. As a consequence, we obtain conditions ensuring the existence of connecting orbits between stationary solutions, which in turn yield the existence of nontrivial standing waves\.
