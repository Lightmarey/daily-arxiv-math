**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 在线性非自治严格双曲周期系统满足显式非共振条件时，任意连续周期解属于 $C^l_{per}$。
   - **路线：** 沿特征线积分，把 PDE 化为周期函数空间上的固定点方程；先证明边界传输算子 $I-G_1$ 可逆，再逐阶证明积分算子组合具有所需周期正则性。
2. **主张：** 半线性系统在相应非共振条件下继承 $C^l$ 周期正则性。
   - **路线：** 对时间求导令 $v=u_t$，得到以 $v$ 为未知量的线性化周期双曲系统；反复应用 Theorem 1.2，并由原方程恢复空间导数，归纳提升正则性。
3. **主张：** 拟线性系统也达到相应 $C^l$ 正则性，完全非共振或幂零耦合给出进一步充分条件。
   - **路线：** 时间微分后得到带冻结系数的半线性系统并应用半线性结果；幂零耦合矩阵使迭代链有限，从而避免逐阶共振条件。

**原始英文摘要**

We study higher regularity and its relation to nonresonant behavior for time&#45;periodic solutions of boundary value problems for one&#45;dimensional linear and nonlinear nonautonomous first&#45;order integro&#45;differential strictly hyperbolic systems. The boundary conditions include integral operators and various types of boundary reflections. We prove that continuous and classical solutions have $C^k$&#45;regularity, provided the coefficients are sufficiently smooth and a suitable number of nonresonance conditions is satisfied. In the linear case, these conditions involve the principal coefficients, the diagonal lower&#45;order coefficients, and the boundary reflection coefficients. In the nonlinear case, they also depend on the nonlinearities and on the solution itself. For nonautonomous hyperbolic systems, higher regularity generally requires additional nonresonance conditions, whose number depends on the desired order of differentiability. These conditions are not only sufficient but, in general, also necessary, revealing a distinctive feature of nonautonomous hyperbolic PDEs. By contrast, in the autonomous case, a single nonresonance condition &#40;if one is needed at all&#41; suffices to obtain arbitrarily high regularity. We also identify a class of nonautonomous hyperbolic problems for which no nonresonance conditions are required. In this case, the higher regularity of solutions is determined solely by the regularity of the data. The main technical tool underlying the proofs is an abstract regularity principle formulated in the setting of vector spaces.
