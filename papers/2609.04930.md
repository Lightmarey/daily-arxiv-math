**限制与不确定性**

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 微观异质问题与均质问题均适定且频率稳定。
   - **路线：** 对局部和全局 Helmholtz 问题使用 Fredholm/阻尼稳定性，建立 k 和 $\epsilon $ 的 resolvent 界；均质系数由 cell problem/平均通量确定。
2. **主张：** 局部多尺度问题定义稳定的基函数和离散空间。
   - **路线：** 在每个 coarse patch 上解带异质系数的局部 Helmholtz 问题，利用 Proposition 5.3 的 $\tau $ 条件保证唯一性；拼接局部基函数形成 MHM 空间。
3. **主张：** MHM 空间对均质化解具有近似能力和准最优投影。
   - **路线：** 用局部 corrector 的正交性和 Céa/Strang 型分解，把总误差分成均质化误差、局部逼近误差和 resonance 项；高频常数显式进入估计。
4. **主张：** 离散解满足频率显式的总误差界。
   - **路线：** 将准最优性与 Theorem 4.1 的 homogenization error 合并，在 $kHmax\le \tau $ 下控制两层误差，得到 Theorem 6.5 和 Corollary 6.6。

**原始英文摘要**

We analyze the numerical approximation of time&#45;harmonic scattering by highly heterogeneous penetrable obstacles. These problems are especially challenging in the high&#45;frequency regime, where the size of the scatterer $L$ is much larger than the wavelength, i.e., the wavenumber $k$ is such that $kL \gg 1$. Here, we further consider the situation where the scatterer contains different materials, with a characteristic size $\varepsilon$ such that $k\varepsilon \ll 1$. We propose a high&#45;order multiscale finite element method, and provide an error analysis that is explicit in both $k$ and $\varepsilon$. Crucially, our error estimates suggest that using a high&#45;order method should reduce the computational cost for large frequencies, which is corroborated by numerical examples.
