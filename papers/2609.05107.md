**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 扇形凸域上的梯度流产生可比较的单调量。
   - **路线：** 沿由共形/梯度方向定义的流线对能量积分求导，边界项由凸性和角度条件控制为非负，得到随半径单调的频率或能量比。
2. **主张：** 正曲率曲面上的局部解具有相同的单调性结构。
   - **路线：** 在局部坐标中将 harmonic/free&#45;boundary 方程变为平面模型加曲率误差，使用 Green/Bochner 恒等式将误差吸收入曲率下界，得到 Theorem 3 的局部解控制。
3. **主张：** 共形几何和梯度线分析控制全局增长。
   - **路线：** 将曲面 conformally 等价到复平面，研究 $log|\nabla R|$ 的 Laplacian 与高斯曲率关系；利用 harmonic function 的 Liouville 性和增长界排除非平凡逃逸。
4. **主张：** 单调性与增长控制推出自由边界的定量结论。
   - **路线：** 把局部 monotonicity 延伸到全局，结合 measure concentration 和 Theorems 16&#45;&#45;20 的比较函数，得到两相问题的增长/分类估计。

**原始英文摘要**

Inspired by the monotonicity formula of Alt, Caffarelli, and Friedman, we consider a natural variant of the ACF functional in positively curved 2&#45;dimensional settings where we integrate over sublevel sets of Green's function rather than disks. We prove sharp almost&#45;monotonicity formulas for our new functional in the case of convex planar domains &#40;both with the pole on the boundary and in the interior&#41; and in the setting of 2&#45;dimensional complete manifolds with Euclidean volume growth and nonnegative Gaussian curvature. The tools include the Schwarz&#45;Christoffel formula and Riesz decomposition using conformal coordinates. As a consequence, using quasiconformal estimates, we give a new Lipschitz bound in the manifold setting for minimizers of the two&#45;phase free boundary problem of Alt, Caffarelli, and Friedman, with the constant depending on only the total integral curvature, in contrast to work of Teixeira and Zhang, which gives constants depending on pointwise bounds for the Riemann curvature tensor and its derivatives. Our methods also recover the Lipschitz bound up to a Neumann boundary of Gemmer, Moon, and Raynor in the planar convex domain case.
