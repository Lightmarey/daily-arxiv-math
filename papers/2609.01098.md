**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Propositions 2\.1–2\.4 将大尺度 Faber–Krahn 不等式转成局部 Sobolev、non-collapsing、volume doubling 和 ℓ2–ℓp 半群界。
   - **路线：** 先对有限支撑函数使用层析/截断推导 Sobolev 不等式，再将球内体积下界与 doubling 递归结合；Moser 时空迭代把这些几何不等式转成热半群的范数衰减。
2. **主张：** Theorem 2\.6 和 Theorem 2\.9 通过积分 Davies 方法得到平均热半群的离对角 Gaussian 衰减。
   - **路线：** 对热方程乘以指数权函数并在时间上积分，优化 Lipschitz 权函数与平均时间窗口；Proposition 2\.7–2\.8 控制权函数共轭后的 Dirichlet 形式，Theorem 2\.9 选择连接 x、y 的最优尺度。
3. **主张：** Theorem 2\.11 将上述局部估计组合为 Faber–Krahn 到平均 Gaussian 上界的充分条件，并推出 Theorem 1\.1。
   - **路线：** 把 Sobolev/doubling 产生的 ℓ2–ℓp 界代回固定时间平均估计，再用时间分片和体积比率控制远离对角的指数项；令外层半径趋于无穷即得到引言中的全局大尺度结论。
4. **主张：** Theorems 3\.1、3\.5 和 3\.6 从平均半群上界反推出 Dirichlet 特征值下界及相对 Faber–Krahn 不等式。
   - **路线：** 先用平均半群作用于集合指示函数得到 L2 衰减，再把谱分解中的最低特征值隔离出来；以相对测度的子集选择和反 doubling 控制体积，优化时间参数后得到 $\\lambda \(U\)$ 的 Faber–Krahn 下界。

**原始英文摘要**

Characterizations of pointwise Gaussian upper bounds on graphs with possibly unbounded geometry in terms of localized functional inequalities contain errors depending on the vertex degree\. We introduce a new space-time averaged form of the heat semigroup in terms of time-averaged $\\ell^\{q/\(q-1\)\}-\\ell^q$-estimates and obtain Gaussian upper bounds from large-scale Faber-Krahn inequalities which avoid such errors\. A main analytic ingredient is an integrated version of Davies' method which yields off-diagonal estimates for this averaged quantity, and which tends to pointwise Gaussian bounds as time tends to infinity\. Conversely, on large scales volume doubling and Gaussian bounds on this averaged heat semigroup norms imply relative Faber-Krahn inequalities for subsets of prescribed relative measure\. Our proof is based on a lower bound for Dirichlet eigenvalues in terms of these averages\. The Faber-Krahn dimension is scale-dependent, but converges to the doubling dimension for increasing radii\. This gives a characterization of asymptotic Gaussian heat kernel behavior in terms of Faber-Krahn inequalities\.
