**原始英文摘要**

The purpose of this work is to provide a broader framework for analyzing the inhomogeneous nonlinear Schrödinger equation &#40;INLS&#41; 

$$iu_t + u_{xx} \pm |x|^{-b}|u|^{α-1}u=0, \quad 1<α< 5-2b\; \text{and}\; 0< b\leq 1/4.$$

 Specifically, we establish low&#45;regularity well&#45;posedness in the Fourier&#45;Lebesgue $\widehat{L}^{p}$ spaces for $4/3<p<8$. The analysis is carried out differently for the cases $p<2$ and $p>2$. Primarily, in both cases, we prove global well&#45;posedness for arbitrarily large initial data via the data decomposition method adapted for the Fourier&#45;Lebesgue spaces. Furthermore, we obtain analogous results for INLS in modulation spaces $M^{p,p'}$ for $4/3<p<2$.
