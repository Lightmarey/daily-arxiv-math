**完成的工作**

我们在具有非负 Ricci 曲率的闭黎曼流形上证明了归一化抛物 $p$-拉普拉斯方程 $u\_t = Δu \+ \(p-2\)\|\\nabla u\|^\{-2\}\\nabla^2u\(\\nabla u,\\nabla u\)$、$1&lt;p&lt;\\infty$ 正解的著名 Li-Yau 不等式的尖锐非线性版本，该方程在解的临界集上以粘度意义进行解释。即使在闭流形类中，该常数也是最好的：它是通过平面 $\\Rn$ 中的显式自相似解来获得的，并且其锐度通过沿平面环面 $\\mathbb R^n/\(L\\mathbb Z\)^n$、$L \\to \\infty$ 的大环面极限转移到紧凑设置。

**使用技术**

- 最大值原理
- Bochner 恒等式
- 广义 Newton 不等式
- 均匀抛物正则化
- 黏性解稳定性

**可能的突破**

正文证明把尖锐 Li--Yau 常数统一保持在正则化极限中，并去除了对解临界集的非退化假设；随后以大平坦环面极限验证常数锐性。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 闭黎曼流形上非负 Ricci 曲率下，归一化抛物型 $p$-Laplacian 的正黏性解满足锐 Li--Yau 不等式。
   - **路线：** 先令 $v=\\log u$，对 Li--Yau 泛函应用最大值原理；在非临界点用 Bochner 恒等式和广义 Newton 不等式得到强制性估计。
2. **主张：** 估计可去除临界集非退化假设，并传递到原始黏性方程。
   - **路线：** 构造只正则化二阶项、将一阶项解耦的均匀抛物近似流；证明精确 Bochner 恒等式和统一锐估计，再令正则化参数趋于零，利用局部一致收敛及黏性超解稳定性。
3. **主张：** 常数是最优的，并可推出全局 Harnack 不等式。
   - **路线：** 用 Euclidean 自相似极值剖面并取大平坦环面极限证明锐性；将 Li--Yau 不等式沿时空曲线积分，完成平方并对曲线作用量取最小值得到 Harnack 估计。

**原始英文摘要**

We prove a sharp nonlinear version of the celebrated Li-Yau inequality for positive solutions of the normalized parabolic $p$-Laplacian equation $u\_t = Δu \+ \(p-2\)\|\\nabla u\|^\{-2\}\\nabla^2u\(\\nabla u,\\nabla u\)$, $1&lt;p&lt;\\infty$, on a closed Riemannian manifold with nonnegative Ricci curvature, the equation being interpreted in the viscosity sense on the critical set of the solution\. The constant is best possible, even within the class of closed manifolds: it is attained identically by an explicit self-similar solution in flat $\\Rn$, and its sharpness transfers to the compact setting through a large-torus limit along the flat tori $\\mathbb R^n/\(L\\mathbb Z\)^n$, $L \\to \\infty$\. The proof rests on an exact Bochner-type identity for a family of uniformly parabolic approximating flows in which the second-order regularization and the first-order eikonal term are decoupled: for this family the maximum principle applies with the sharp constant, uniformly in the regularization parameter, and with no assumption on the critical set of the solution\. The identity is moreover form-invariant under the classical $α$-relaxation of the Li-Yau functional; as a consequence we also obtain the corresponding inequality on closed manifolds with $\\operatorname\{Ric\} \\ge -κ$ \(again with no assumption beyond the Ricci lower bound\), and, on complete noncompact manifolds with $\\operatorname\{Ric\} \\ge 0$, the sharp inequality for the approximating flows -- again with no assumption on the critical set -- under a cutoff hypothesis on the distance function \(automatic in $\\Rn$, and, for $p \\ge 2$, under nonnegative sectional curvature\) and a qualitative polynomial growth condition on the Li-Yau quantity, satisfied, with exponent zero, by the extremal profile\. A sharp global Harnack inequality follows\.
