**可能的突破**

Theorem 2\.2 给出三种互补坐标规模下的精确参数区：分别为 $s\+\\alpha &lt;1、s\+\\alpha \+\\beta &lt;1、s\+max\{\\alpha \+\\beta ,2\\beta \}&lt;1；Theorem$ 2\.3 将结论同时施加到每个占据自旋块，Corollary 2\.8 说明这些区域在库仑 Hamiltonian 类上统一尖锐。

**限制与不确定性**

2. **主张：** 反对称性使电子-电子 pair operator 的允许目标权重从普通阈值 t&lt;−1 提升到 $-1\\le t&lt;0$，并获得一阶增益。
   - **路线：** 在 Section 3\.2 用交换变量写出差分核 $1/\|k\|^\{2\}-1/\|k-\(x-y\)\|^\{2\}$；对其作加权 L1 积分并控制差分核，得到反对称输入的乘积权重估计。
3. **主张：** 加权库仑估计传递给 resolvent，形成满足\(2\.2\)的正 Fourier 权重增益。

**原始英文摘要**

We establish sharp mixed spectral Barron regularity for eigenfunctions of molecular Coulomb Hamiltonians\. The mixed norm is a Fourier $L^1$ norm with one isotropic weight and coordinate-product weights, and therefore detects regularity invisible to the isotropic Barron scale\. For a nonempty set $I$ of electron indices on which the wave function is antisymmetric, we derive an explicit admissible region for the isotropic order $s$ and the coordinate orders $α,β$\. This region is optimal as a uniform statement over the class of clamped-nuclei Coulomb Hamiltonians\. For fixed-spin components with two occupied spin blocks, it reduces to $s\+α\+β&lt;1$; in the fully spin-polarized class it reduces to $s\+α&lt;1$\. In particular, if $\\mathcal I\_σ$ denotes the family of occupied same-spin blocks determined by $σ$, then every fixed-spin spatial component $ψ\_σ$ satisfies, for every $0\\leqα&lt;1$, \\\[ \\left\(\\sum\_\{I\\in\\mathcal I\_σ\}\\prod\_\{i\\in I\}\\langleξ\_i\\rangle^α\\right\)\\widehat\{ψ\_σ\}\\in L^1\(\\mathbb\{R\}^\{3N\}\)\. \\\] For a fully spin-polarized state, $\\mathcal I\_σ=\\\{\\\{1,\\ldots,N\\\}\\\}$\.
