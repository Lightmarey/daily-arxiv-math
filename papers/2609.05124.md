2. **主张：** 分数阶跳跃核决定传播前沿的对数尺度。
   - **路线：** 估计半群核的多项式尾部并构造 level&#45;set 上下界，平衡反应增长 $e^{at}$ 与跳跃尾部 &#124;x&#124;−d−2s，得到 radius 的指数时间关系。
3. **主张：** 紧支撑初值的 level set 具有 a/&#40;d&#43;2s&#41; 的传播率。

**原始英文摘要**

Let $P$ denote normalized nearest&#45;neighbor averaging on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and set $A=I-P$. We consider $\partial_tu+A^su=f(u),t>0, x\in\mathbb{Z}^d, 0<s<1.$ For a Fisher&#45;KPP reaction with $a=f'(0)>0$, compactly supported data have logarithmic propagation rate $a/(d+2s)$. In the logistic case, each fixed level set lies between two constant multiples of $\exp(at/(d+2s))$; this localization remains valid for data with the critical algebraic tail. Nondecreasing front&#45;like data in one dimension instead propagate at rate $a/(2s)$, and the resulting acceleration rules out planar traveling fronts of finite speed in every dimension. We also analyze the singular limit $s\uparrow1$. If $τ_s=-\log(1-s)$, then throughout $at<τ_s$ compactly supported solutions follow the Wulff shape of the local nearest&#45;neighbor equation. After a constant multiple of $τ_s$, their propagation is exponential. Half&#45;space data exhibit the corresponding transition from the directional local speed to the exponent $a/(2s)$. The analysis rests on sharp bounds for the subordinated kernel, algebraic barriers, a compactness&#45;&#45;Liouville argument, and a decomposition of the fractional walk into its nearest&#45;neighbor and rare long&#45;jump parts.
