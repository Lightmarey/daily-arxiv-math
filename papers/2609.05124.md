**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 离散分数阶算子生成正保序的全局解。
   - **路线：** 用 Fourier/随机游走表示 $A^s$ 的半群，建立最大值原理和 $\\ell\\infty $ 稳定性；比较原理给出初值序关系和单调性。
2. **主张：** 分数阶跳跃核决定传播前沿的对数尺度。
   - **路线：** 估计半群核的多项式尾部并构造 level-set 上下界，平衡反应增长 $e^\{at\}$ 与跳跃尾部 \|x\|−d−2s，得到 radius 的指数时间关系。
3. **主张：** 紧支撑初值的 level set 具有 a/\(d\+2s\) 的传播率。
   - **路线：** 用近场 supersolution 控制上界，以 subsolution 和 entire-solution Liouville 引理排除前沿后方空洞；对任意方向和阈值取极限得到 Theorems 2\.3、2\.4、2\.8。
4. **主张：** 当 s↑1 时非局部传播过渡到局部 Fisher--KPP，且无经典平面波。
   - **路线：** 对离散核系数作 s→1 的渐近，控制时间尺度 $\\tau s$；再用方向性线性传播速度和反证 entire front 的尾部行为，证明 Theorem 9\.4。

**原始英文摘要**

Let $P$ denote normalized nearest-neighbor averaging on $\\mathbb\{Z\}^d$ and set $A=I-P$\. We consider $\\partial\_tu\+A^su=f\(u\),t&gt;0, x\\in\\mathbb\{Z\}^d, 0&lt;s&lt;1\.$ For a Fisher-KPP reaction with $a=f'\(0\)&gt;0$, compactly supported data have logarithmic propagation rate $a/\(d\+2s\)$\. In the logistic case, each fixed level set lies between two constant multiples of $\\exp\(at/\(d\+2s\)\)$; this localization remains valid for data with the critical algebraic tail\. Nondecreasing front-like data in one dimension instead propagate at rate $a/\(2s\)$, and the resulting acceleration rules out planar traveling fronts of finite speed in every dimension\. We also analyze the singular limit $s\\uparrow1$\. If $τ\_s=-\\log\(1-s\)$, then throughout $at&lt;τ\_s$ compactly supported solutions follow the Wulff shape of the local nearest-neighbor equation\. After a constant multiple of $τ\_s$, their propagation is exponential\. Half-space data exhibit the corresponding transition from the directional local speed to the exponent $a/\(2s\)$\. The analysis rests on sharp bounds for the subordinated kernel, algebraic barriers, a compactness--Liouville argument, and a decomposition of the fractional walk into its nearest-neighbor and rare long-jump parts\.
