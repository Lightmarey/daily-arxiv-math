**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Theorem 2.3 将一阶自由边界平坦化后的最小子余项提升到局部 Hölder 控制。
   - **路线：** 用 Euler–Lagrange 方程在 $|\nabla u|>1$ 与 $|\nabla u|<1$ 两相分别线性化，Lemma 2.1 的局部 Sobolev 正则性和边界条件控制两侧梯度；紧集上的估计使一阶平面斜率可稳定选取。
2. **主张：** Lemmas 3.1–3.3 和 Theorem 3.9 构造 broken polynomial p 并识别二阶投影余项。
   - **路线：** 对最小子序列以 $L^{2}$ 投影消去常数、线性项和两侧二次矩，Lemma 3.1–3.3 给出归一化余项的两相 PDE；Definition 3.5 的 broken&#45;polynomial 类保留自由边界跳跃，Theorem 3.9 给出序列收敛到其中的 p。
3. **主张：** Section 4.2 的 $q^j$ 正则性理论证明极限 $q^0$ 在两侧为 $C^3$。
   - **路线：** 用 $L^{2}$ 投影后的方程把 $q^j$ 的系数误差写成 $\delta _j$ 的余项，内外半球分别应用 $Schauder/W^{2,2}$ 提升；统一界和弱收敛允许在任意 $B_r^\pm $ 中迭代到三阶连续性。
4. **主张：** Section 4.3 的主定理把 p 与 $q^0$ 拼成第三阶渐近展开。
   - **路线：** 将 Section 4.2 的两侧 $C^3$ 极限代回原能量的一阶变分，控制 $q^j-q^0$ 的 $L^{2}$ 余项；Theorem 4.17 的重标度恢复 $u=a+\nu \cdot x+\delta p+\delta \epsilon q$，并给出自由边界后续正则化所需的展开。

**原始英文摘要**

This is the first part of a project concerning the regularity of the free boundary of a function, where the free boundary depends on the gradient of the function. We study the minimizer of the expression 

$$J(u) : = \int_{B_1} F(\nabla u) \ dx,$$

 where $F(x)$ is a uniformly convex function whose second derivatives might jump at $|x|=1$. This results in an Euler&#45;Lagrange equation that varies over the free boundary, which we define as $$ \Gamma = \partial \{x \in B_1: |\nabla u| >1 \} \cap \partial \{x \in B_1: |\nabla u| <1 \}. $$ We consider two&#45;phase flat points for which the second derivatives of $F$ jump over $\Gamma$. In this paper, we show that under some regularity and non&#45;degeneracy assumptions, a minimizer $u$ can be expressed as $$ u(x)= a + \nu \cdot x+ \delta p(x) + \delta \epsilon q(x), $$ where $a \in \mathbb{R}$, $\nu \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $0 < \delta, \epsilon << 1$. Here $p$ is a $C^1$ function, which consists of one polynomial in the upper half ball and another polynomial in the lower half ball. The function $q$ is a rest term with bounded $L^2$ norm. Furthermore, assuming that we have a sequence, $u^j$, of minimizers, we show that $q^j$ converges weakly to a function $q^0$ that satisfies a certain PDE. In addition, we show that $q^0$ is $C^3$ in $B_r^+$ and $B_r^-$, respectively, for $0<r<1$. This is the main result of this paper which is intended to be used to show regularity of the free boundary.
