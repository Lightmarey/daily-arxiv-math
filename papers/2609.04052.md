**可能的突破**

把 Caffarelli 收缩估计从有界 Hessian 的光滑情形扩展到全空间 Lipschitz 扰动，并以方向性梯度宽度保留各向异性信息，得到维数无关的两侧度量控制。

**限制与不确定性**

需要严格凸高斯基准和全局 Lipschitz 扰动；混合测度应用还依赖紧支撑和归一化条件，结论是 Brenier 映射的定量估计而非一般非凸输运正则性。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 平衡坐标中的两个高斯势满足 Brenier 映射的谱 Hessian 上界。
   - **路线：** 对最优传输势的线性化方程使用方向性梯度宽度，在最大值点比较二阶差分；矩阵 Schur 补将交叉项压入基准协方差，得到方向性谱包络。
2. **主张：** 上述 Hessian 控制在仅 Lipschitz 扰动下仍可通过逼近保持。
   - **路线：** 先对扰动势作光滑卷积，利用全空间正则性和一致大尺度模估计得到统一紧性；再用 Brenier 势的局部一致收敛与 Hessian 闭性把估计传到极限。
3. **主张：** 谱上界可转化为双向 Brenier 映射的双 Lipschitz/度量估计。
   - **路线：** 对正向和逆向输运分别应用谱扰动定理，以凸共轭交换源目标；将两侧 Hessian 界积分成距离估计，得到 Theorem 5.1 的 forward&#45;&#45;reverse transfer。
4. **主张：** 高斯混合的位移范围与 Hessian 下界可由同一惩罚函数框架得到。
   - **路线：** 对混合测度中心化，利用支撑宽度构造径向惩罚并在联合最大值处应用 Schur coercivity；结合反向收缩和 Theorem 6.3，得到 Theorem 6.1 的位移与曲率结论。

**原始英文摘要**

We study dimension&#45;free differential estimates for Brenier maps under first&#45;order perturbations of the two marginals. Caffarelli's contraction theorem yields such estimates when the source and target potentials satisfy a pointwise Hessian comparison. We show that the same conclusion remains true under arbitrary globally Lipschitz perturbations of both marginals, without additional assumptions. In particular, this answers the conjecture due to Fathi, Mikulincer, and Shenfeld. More precisely, let $d\ge1$, $L\ge0$, and let $B:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ be globally $L$&#45;Lipschitz. We prove that the Brenier map from the standard Gaussian measure $γ_d$ to the probability measure proportional to $\mathrm{e}^{-B}γ_d$ has a globally Lipschitz representative whose bound depends only on $L$. The Brenier potential also belongs to $C^{1,1}(\mathbb{R}^d)$ and satisfies dimension&#45;free two&#45;sided Hessian bounds. Let $\mathfrak{C}(0,L)$ denote the upper&#45;bound constant, then 

$$\log\mathfrak{C}(0,L)=4L^2+\log L+\mathcal{O}(1) \qquad(L\to\infty),$$

 and a one&#45;dimensional example with Brenier map $T_L$ satisfies $\log\operatorname{Lip}(T_L)\ge L^2/2$. We deduce this Gaussian estimate from an anisotropic two&#45;marginal result. Let $V,W:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ be potentials, and let $Q,P$ be positive&#45;definite matrices. Using the distributional curvature bounds $D^2V\preceq Q$ and $D^2W\succeq P$, we establish matrix Hessian bounds for Brenier maps between arbitrary globally Lipschitz perturbations of the reference marginals. Most importantly, the constants keep the directional geometry of the essential gradient ranges. For affine perturbations, our estimates recover the sharp noncommuting Caffarelli tensor. We further obtain pointwise displacement bounds for Gaussian perturbations and spectral Hessian estimates for compact Gaussian mixtures.
