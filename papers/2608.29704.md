**完成的工作**

论文从无粘稳态 Burgers 解 $u0u0'=f$ 出发，递归构造粘性方程的形式展开系数 u1,…,un，并证明小粘性下存在周期解且余项为 $O\(\\epsilon ^\{n\+1\}\)$。核心是把余项改写为长区间上的 Riccati 型初值问题，用上下解得到全区间有界性，再以隐函数定理调节初始斜率实现周期闭合。

**使用技术**

**可能的突破**

论文把任意满足 $u0u0'=f$ 且保持负值的光滑周期无粘解，系统地提升为小粘性稳态周期解，并把任意阶渐近展开的误差控制归约为一个可调初始斜率的全局 Riccati 闭合问题。

**限制与不确定性**

构造要求无粘基态 u0 严格有界在负半轴并满足相应光滑周期条件$，\\epsilon $ 只需足够小且阈值依赖展开阶数；结果是存在与渐近误差估计，没有讨论多分支选择、非负基态或大粘性行为。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 式\(1\.7\) 的递归关系确定每个高阶系数 uk，并使截断展开满足稳态 Burgers 方程直到 $\\epsilon ^n$ 阶。
   - **路线：** 把 $u\\epsilon =u0\+\\epsilon u1\+…$ 代入 $-\\epsilon u''\+uu'=f$，按 $\\epsilon $ 的幂次比较$；u0u0'=f$ 消去零阶项，因 u0 不为零，逐阶用一阶线性方程解出 uk 的周期光滑解。
2. **主张：** Theorem 1\.4 将截断展开的余项转化为长区间 $\[0,2\\pi /\\epsilon \]$ 上有界的周期 Riccati 型问题。
   - **路线：** 令 $u\\epsilon =\\Sigma \_\{j=0\}^n$ $\\epsilon ^j$ $uj\+\\epsilon ^\{n\+1\}g\(x/\\epsilon \)$，整理得到式\(1\.9\)；再将周期条件写成 $g\(0\)=g\(2\\pi /\\epsilon \)$，把问题等价为带初值 $\\alpha \_\{0\}、\\beta \_\{0\}$ 的二阶 Cauchy 方程和一个闭合残差 $\\Phi \(\\epsilon ,\\beta \_\{0\}\)=0$。
3. **主张：** Lemma 3\.3 证明余项初值问题在整个半轴存在，并给出与 $\\lambda $ 无关的上下界。
   - **路线：** 将一阶化方程用积分因子变为 w' 方程，利用 $u0\+\\lambda ^\{2\}u1\+…$ 保持严格负值；比较 w'与 $w^\{2\}$ 的上解以及指数下解，得到全局存在，再由微分不等式在 g 低于阈值时强制 g'&gt;0，闭合区间 \[−2M1/m0,0\) 的统一界。
4. **主张：** Theorem 3\.1 通过隐函数定理选择 $\\beta \_\{0\}$，使余项周期闭合，从而推出 Theorem 1\.3 的 $O\(\\epsilon ^\{n\+1\}\)$ 周期解。
   - **路线：** 比较 $g\(y,\\beta \_\{0\}\)$ 与 $\\lambda =0$ 的显式极限解，证明闭合残差 $\\Phi $ 连续可微且在 $\\lambda =0$ 对 $\\beta \_\{0\}$ 的导数非零；隐函数定理给出 $\\beta \_\{0\}\(\\lambda \)$ 使 $\\Phi =0$，统一界再把 g 回代到原变量，得到周期性和余项估计。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we will construct periodic solutions to the viscous steady Burgers equation with an external force $f\(x\)$, based on the following formal expansion $u^\\varepsilon\(x\)=u\_0\(x\)\+\\varepsilon u\_1\(x\)\+\\cdots\+\\varepsilon^n u\_n\(x\)\+\\cdots$, where $\\varepsilon \\ge 0$ represents the viscosity and $u\_0\(x\)$ is a solution to non-viscous steady Burgers equation with the external force $f\(x\)$\. We will focus on the solutions which are uniformly bounded with respect to the viscosity\. In our previous work, starting from $u\_0=-\(2\+\\cos x\)$, the authors constructed the periodic solutions to the viscous steady Burgers equation with the external force $f=u\_0\(x\)u\_\{0x\}=-2\\sin x-\\sin x\\cos x$\. In this paper, we will extend the main result obtained in our previous work and construct the solutions starting from general $u\_0$ and $f$ satisfying the non-viscous steady Burgers equation $u\_0\(x\)u\_\{0x\}=f$\. It will be shown that there exists a $\\varepsilon\_0&gt;0$, which depends on $n$, such that for any $0&lt;\\varepsilon&lt;\\varepsilon\_0$, the viscous steady Burgers equation with the external force $f$ has a periodic solution $u^\\varepsilon\(x\) \\in C^2\(\[0,2π\]\)$, satisfying $\|u^\\varepsilon\(x\)-u\_0\(x\)-\\varepsilon u\_1\(x\)-\\cdots-\\varepsilon^n u\_n\(x\)\| \\leq C\\varepsilon^\{n\+1\}$, where $C&gt;0$ is a constant which depends on $n$, but is independent of $\\varepsilon$\. Compared with Jauslin-Kreiss-Moser's result, we present a new approach to construct periodic solutions to the viscous steady Burgers with a general external force\. The constructed solutions will tend to the ones of the non-viscous Burgers equation with a sharper convergence rate \(up to higher order\) when the viscosity vanishes\.
