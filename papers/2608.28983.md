**原始英文摘要**

We establish Schauder estimates for linearized very fast diffusion equations with Dirichlet boundary conditions in bounded smooth domains. These estimates provide an important ingredient for deriving optimal boundary regularity and long&#45;time dynamics of solutions to very fast diffusion equations.
