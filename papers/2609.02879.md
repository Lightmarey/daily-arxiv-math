**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 正则点等价于近端次微分为单点，因而 cut 点可由 $\partial^P\!\phi(x)=\varnothing$ 判定。
   - **路线：** 从光滑函数在下方接触推出可微和唯一近端梯度，再用半凹性控制任意超微分；反向用局部二次接触把唯一近端梯度提升为正则性。
2. **主张：** 正则度 $R_\phi(x)$ 与 cut time $\tau_{\phi,H}(x)$ 互相控制，并且接触 cut locus 与变分 cut locus 一致。
   - **路线：** 在有校准曲线的时间内用 Lax&#45;&#45;Oleinik 动作函数作下方接触，得到 $R\lesssim1/\tau$；反向从有限正则度的二次接触沿 Hamiltonian 流推进，利用动作函数局部半凸性构造最小点，得到 $\tau\gtrsim1/R$，再取等价定义。
3. **主张：** 校准曲线与广义特征之间满足定量分离估计，从而 cut locus 为零测集并可简化证明 Alexandrov 定理。
   - **路线：** 将广义特征的微分包含与校准曲线的 Hamilton 方程相减，用正则度估计超梯度差，再对距离微分不等式使用双向 Gronwall；随后以半群平移和可测微分集覆盖，把正则度有限的部分与 cut 集的零测性结合。
4. **主张：** cut 点沿广义特征向未来传播，Alexandrov 点沿校准曲线传播并满足矩阵 Riccati 演化。
   - **路线：** 先用时间切片正则点等价和半群保序性识别演化方程的接触状态；在校准曲线上利用 Hamiltonian 流的线性化和二阶动作 Hessian，微分 Riccati 恒等式，结合 Theorem 3.6 的分离避免共轭点，得到传播结论及闭性判据。

**原始英文摘要**

For a semiconcave function with linear modulus, we introduce the cut locus through a touching approach and prove that it coincides with the variational cut locus defined via the Lax&#45;&#45;Oleinik semigroup, independently of the Hamiltonian. Cut points are characterized by the emptiness of the proximal subdifferential of $φ$, which we use as the analytic criterion behind the touching definition. We introduce the degree of regularity $R_φ$, which measures the $C^{1,1}$ deviation of $φ$, and prove the quantitative relation $R_φ(x)\sim 1/τ_{φ,H}(x)$ with the cut time function, valid pointwise up to explicit truncation constants. From this relation we derive a separation estimate for calibrated curves, showing that no conjugate points occur before the cut locus. This estimate supplies the tools for the propagation results. Cut points propagate globally along generalized characteristics for the evolutionary Hamilton&#45;&#45;Jacobi equation, and Alexandrov points propagate forward along calibrated curves, with the second derivative satisfying a matrix Riccati equation. We also show that the cut locus is a Lebesgue null set and give a streamlined proof of Alexandrov's theorem. Finally, we give necessary and sufficient conditions for the cut locus of a weak KAM solution to be closed, in terms of the cut time function, the $C^{1,1}$ support, and the degree of regularity.
