**完成的工作**

本文在紧致带边界 Riemann 流形上研究含主动 constitutive 项 $a\(\\Delta \_g$ $u\)\|\\nabla \_g$ u\| 的非变分 Cahn–Hilliard 演化。作者先对 Galerkin 解建立能量、时间导数和双调和 coercivity 估计，关键地以 classifier 与 anchoring law 的单调性作单侧比较，获得 $\\Delta \_g$ $u\_m\\to \\Delta \_g u$ 的强 L2 紧性，从而在普通弱形式中识别主动通量。二维时 Agmon 不等式使立方双稳势差可控，给出唯一性和稳定性；对 $a\_N\(r\)=2\\pi ^\{-1\}arctan\(Nr\)$ 的陡峭化序列，则提取 $Sign\(\\Delta \_g$ u\) 图的弱极限，在二维进一步由状态唯一性把子序列收敛提升为全序列收敛。

**使用技术**

- Galerkin 逼近与双调和 coercivity
- Aubin–Lions 紧性
- 单调性驱动的 Minty/一侧比较
- 二维 Agmon 不等式稳定性
- 最大单调 sign-graph 极限

**可能的突破**

关键突破是用 Galerkin 层面的单侧单调比较恢复二阶强紧性，避免主动项在仅有弱 $\\Delta \_g u$ 紧性下产生缺陷；因此既能识别平滑 classifier 的普通弱解，也能在 classifier 斜率发散时得到 $Sign\(\\Delta \_g$ u\) 微分包含，并区分二维状态唯一与乘子 $\\xi $ 可能非唯一。

**限制与不确定性**

存在性允许任意维数但要求 q&gt;6、紧致流形/边界条件和文中 b 的 coercive growth 假设；唯一性与连续依赖只在 $d=2。sign-graph$ 极限一般只有子序列和乘子弱收敛$，\\xi $ 在 $\{\\Delta \_g$ $u=0\}$ 可非唯一；数值 flat-torus 实验不替代解析结论。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Galerkin 近似具有统一 $L2、H\_D^2$ 和 Lq 界，并可提取普通弱解的强低阶收敛。
   - **路线：** 测试有限维方程得到双调和项的正性、classifier 有界性和 anchoring coercivity 的能量界；时间导数落在对偶空间，Aubin–Lions 随后给出 $u\_m$ 在 $H^1/L6$ 等空间的强收敛。
2. **主张：** 通过单侧比较得到 $\\Delta \_g$ $u\_m\\to \\Delta \_g u$ 强收敛，并识别主动产品 $a\(\\Delta \_g$ $u\_m\)\|\\nabla u\_m\|$ 的极限。
   - **路线：** 将 Galerkin 解与同时空正则化投影 $P\_m$ $u^\\zeta $ 比较，以差值测试；classifier 单调性使 $\(a\(\\Delta u\_m\)-a\(\\Delta P\_m$ $u^\\zeta \)\)\(\\Delta u\_m-\\Delta P\_m$ $u^\\zeta \)\|\\nabla u\_m\|\\ge 0$，双调和项吸收剩余，先取 $m\\to \\infty $ 再取 $\\zeta \\to 0$。
3. **主张：** 在二维，平滑 classifier 问题的弱解唯一且连续依赖初值和参考态。
   - **路线：** 对两个弱解作差并用差值测试；二维 Agmon 嵌入把 W′\(u\)−W′\(v\) 的立方项控制为 L2 差乘 $H\_D^2$ 范数，主动项只需 a 的有界性，随后 Gronwall 关闭稳定性估计。
4. **主张：** 陡峭 classifier $a\_N$ 收敛到 $Sign\(\\Delta \_g$ u\) 图，且二维状态 u 的整个序列收敛。
   - **路线：** 统一估计给出 subsequence 的强 $\\Delta \_g$ 收敛和 $a\_N\(\\Delta u\_N\)$ 的弱星界；在 $\\Delta u&gt;0/&lt;0$ 集合上用点态饱和识别 $\\xi =\\pm 1$，在零集上保留 $\|\\xi \|\\le 1$，形成 $\\xi \\in Sign\(\\Delta u\)$。二维 Theorem 3\.5 的状态唯一性再把子序列结论升级为全序列。

**原始英文摘要**

We establish a weak solution theory and a singular constitutive limit for a non variational Cahn Hilliard evolution on compact Riemannian manifolds, possibly with boundary\. The model combines the classical double well chemical potential with an active correction concentrated in diffuse transition layers and sensitive to the sign of the Laplace Beltrami operator, together with a monotone anchoring mechanism toward a prescribed reference state\. The active correction breaks the passive gradient flow structure and introduces a nonlinear dependence on second derivatives not identified by the natural weak compactness estimates\. For square integrable initial data and sufficiently integrable reference data, we construct weak solutions in arbitrary dimension\. A Galerkin level one sided comparison argument, using monotonicity of the classifier and anchoring law together with biharmonic coercivity, yields strong convergence of the approximate Laplacians and hence strong second order compactness\. This identifies the nonlinear active term in an ordinary weak formulation, without a second derivative defect\. In two dimensions we prove uniqueness and continuous dependence\. The estimates depend only on the bound and monotonicity of the classifier, not on its slope, and are therefore uniform for increasingly steep arctangent classifiers\. Their singular limit is governed by the maximal monotone sign graph acting on the Laplace Beltrami operator\. In arbitrary dimension we obtain subsequential strong second order convergence to a weak solution of the resulting differential inclusion\. In two dimensions the limiting state is unique, so the entire steep classifier family converges; uniqueness of the constitutive multiplier on the zero Laplacian set is not asserted\. This is a constitutive steepness limit at fixed diffuse interface thickness, rather than a sharp interface limit\.
