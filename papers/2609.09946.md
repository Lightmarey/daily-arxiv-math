**完成的工作**

论文研究二维不可压 second-grade fluid 通过 Navier-slip 边界切向 traction 控制无扩散标量的最优混合。边界控制先被 lifting 为内部强迫，state system 同时含 g 和时间导数 g；作者证明 passive scalar 的全局状态适定和 active scalar 的控制无关局部适定。随后在终端 $H^\{-1\}$ mix-norm、二次 control cost 和可选 enstrophy reward 下证明最优控制存在，建立 control-to-state 的方向可微性并构造 backward adjoint。对线性化状态和 adjoint 做 duality 得到一阶变分不等式；在 $beta=zeta=0$ 时额外 adjoint 正则性给出最优控制唯一性。

**使用技术**

- Navier-slip boundary lifting
- second-grade fluid energy estimates
- control-to-state directional differentiability
- backward adjoint equation
- duality variational inequality

**可能的突破**

将 boundary traction 控制、二阶流体的非局部状态方程和终端 mix-norm 优化结合，建立从状态适定、最优控制存在到方向导数/adjoint 必要条件的完整控制框架，并在无 friction/enstrophy 权重时得到唯一性。

**限制与不确定性**

active scalar 只在 control-independent local interval 适定；唯一性需要 $beta=zeta=0$ 和额外 adjoint regularity，含一般 friction 或 enstrophy reward 时只保证存在而非唯一。结果针对二维光滑域、Navier-slip 边界和特定 second-grade constitutive law。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 边界 traction lifting 后，passive scalar 状态对 admissible control 全局存在且 energy 有界。
   - **路线：** 把非齐次边界数据写成 divergence-free lifting G，控制项和 $G\_t$ 进入流体方程；对 second-grade generalized vorticity 做能量测试，再用 transport equation 的 L2 梯度估计，得到 control-independent finite-time bounds。
2. **主张：** 控制到状态映射在目标拓扑中存在方向导数。
   - **路线：** 取 controls g\+rh 并减去基准状态，除以 r 后得到线性化 fluid-scalar system；非线性 second-grade terms 的差商由 uniform bounds 控制，remainder 在状态空间中趋零，得到 Lemma 6\.1 和 Theorem 7\.1。
3. **主张：** 最优控制存在并满足 backward adjoint 给出的 first-order variational inequality。
   - **路线：** direct method 先用 control cost coercivity 和状态紧性取得 minimizing sequence 极限，再以线性化方程定义 adjoint；把状态导数与 adjoint 配对，消去 bulk variation，留下 terminal mix-norm derivative 与 control cost 的不等式。
4. **主张：** 当 $beta=zeta=0$ 时最优控制唯一。
   - **路线：** 额外 adjoint stability 使两个最优控制的 terminal variations 可比较；分别对两个 variational inequalities 取对方控制作测试并相加，严格的 fluid dissipation/duality 项迫使控制差为零。

**原始英文摘要**

We study optimal boundary mixing of a nondiffusive scalar transported by a two-dimensional incompressible second-grade fluid\. The control is the tangential traction in a Navier-slip boundary condition, and the principal objective is the terminal $\(H^1\(Ω\)\)'$ mix-norm, supplemented by a quadratic control cost and an optional enstrophy reward\. The second-grade constitutive law introduces a spatially filtered acceleration and a generalized vorticity; after lifting the nonhomogeneous boundary data, both $\\mathbf\{g\}$ and $\\partial\_t\\mathbf\{g\}$ enter the state equation, where $\\mathbf\{g\}$ is the boundary control variable\. We prove global state well-posedness for a passive scalar and well-posedness on a common, control-independent local interval for an active scalar\. We then establish existence of an optimal control, directional differentiability of the control-to-state map in the topology required by the terminal objective, and a weak backward adjoint formulation\. A duality identity yields the first-order variational inequality\. In the absence of the friction coefficient $β$ and enstrophy reward weight $ζ$ \(that is, $β=ζ=0$\), the additional adjoint regularity implies uniqueness of the optimal control for all sufficiently large control penalties $γ$\.
