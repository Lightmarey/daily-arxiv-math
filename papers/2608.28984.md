**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 在边界非退化初值条件下，超快扩散方程存在唯一的全局 admissible 解，并具有最优边界正则性和正性比较估计。
   - **路线：** 先建立有限时间解、比较原理和边界附近的线性距离控制；通过 Schauder 边界正则性估计提升到 $C^{1,p+1}$，再用友好巨人分离变量解作上下屏障并由唯一性拼接全局解。
2. **主张：** 边界正则性指数 $p+1$ 最优。
   - **路线：** 以一维友好巨人解为反例，利用奇异椭圆剖面 $S$ 的边界渐近 $S'(0)-S'(x)\sim x^{p+1}$，排除更高 Hölder 指数。
3. **主张：** 长时间行为由友好巨人剖面主导，并可得到首项修正和衰减估计。
   - **路线：** 采用对数时间和自相似变换，把原方程化为带漂移和反应项的归一化方程；结合边界正则性、紧性和线性化谱或比较估计，识别极限平移并控制余项。

**原始英文摘要**

We prove the optimal global regularity of admissible solutions to a transformed very fast diffusion equation in the range $-1<p<0$, posed on smooth bounded domains with zero Dirichlet boundary data and initial data comparable to the distance function. More precisely, we establish existence and uniqueness and show that solutions belong to $C^{1,p+1}(\overlineΩ)$ in space for every positive time and are $C^\infty$ in time uniformly up to the boundary. Moreover, all their time derivatives belong to $C^{1,p+1}(\overlineΩ)$, and the exponent $p+1$ is optimal. These regularity estimates further yield fine long&#45;time asymptotics toward the friendly giant solution, including a first&#45;order expansion in the $C^{1,p+1}(\overlineΩ)$ topology and an improved convergence rate for the relative error in $C^{p+1}(\overlineΩ)$.
