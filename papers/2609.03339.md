**完成的工作**

研究外部 Serrin 条件下齐次 k-Hessian 方程的刚性：先完成 rank-one Hessian 补全和有限部渐近，再建立外部梯度最大值与边界曲率不等式，最后用 Rellich--Pohozaev 通量恒等式得到边界比值和球形结论。

**使用技术**

- k-Hessian Newton 张量
- 外部 Rellich--Pohozaev 恒等式
- 梯度最大值原理
- 边界曲率不等式
- 有限部渐近

**可能的突破**

将外部衰减解的无穷远质量与有限部严格对应起来，并把 Serrin 边界条件转为边界商恒等式，在次临界、临界和超临界区间统一排除非球形外域。

**限制与不确定性**

域需光滑、有界、严格星形及严格\(k-1\)-凸，且 k 分区间决定不同的无穷远处理；结论不覆盖一般非星形外域。

**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** 外部解的 Hessian/梯度可在无穷远具有可控的有限部展开。
   - **路线：** 以径向模型和 rank-one Hessian completion 处理主导特征值，建立外部解的渐近展开、Newton 张量弱连续性及有限部质量定义。
2. **主张：** Serrin 边界条件迫使外部梯度最大值出现在边界并产生曲率约束。
   - **路线：** 对梯度的 gauge 函数使用最大值原理，结合 Newton 张量的边界收缩建立 boundary curvature inequality，再将恒定法向导数写成边界商。
3. **主张：** 外部区域的通量守恒把边界积分与无穷远有限部联系起来。
   - **路线：** 对 Newton 张量构造的 current 做截断外域积分，应用 divergence theorem；令外半径趋于无穷，vanishing Wronskian flux 消去余项，得到 Rellich--Pohozaev 型恒等式。
4. **主张：** 不同 k 区间的边界比值恒等式与严格凸性共同推出外域刚性。
   - **路线：** 将有限部质量代回 subcritical/critical/supercritical boundary ratio，结合 boundary curvature inequality；等号情形强迫支持函数和曲率常数化，从而域为球并完成 Theorems 1\.5--1\.7。

**排序理由**

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we study the exterior overdetermined problems for the homogeneous k-Hessian equations $$σ\_k\(D^2u\)=0\\quad\\text\{in\}~\\mathbb\{R\}^n\\setminus\\overlineΩ$$ in three dimensional regimes\. For $2\\le k&lt;n$ and smooth strictly star-shaped domain $Ω\\Subset\\R^n$, we establish ball rigidity results in all three dimensional regimes\. If $2\\leq k&lt;\\frac\{n\}\{2\}$, the solution with boundary value $-1$ and limit zero at infinity is treated under strict $\(k-1\)$-convexity of $\\partialΩ$\. If $k=\\frac\{n\}\{2\}$, the solution with boundary value $0$ and logarithmic growth at infinity is considered under strict $\(k-1\)$-convexity of $\\partialΩ$; if $k&gt;\\frac\{n\}\{2\}$, the solution with boundary $1$ and fundamental power-growth at infinity is considered under strict $\(k-1\)$-convexity of $\\partialΩ$\. In each case, for a positive constant $c$, $\|Du\|=c&gt;0$ on $\\partialΩ$ forces $Ω$ to be a Euclidean ball and determines the solution explicitly\. The three arguments are organized by the single parameter $q=\(n-k\)/k$ and the scale function \\\[ F\_q\(t\)=\\frac\{t^\{1-q\}-1\}\{1-q\},\\qquad F\_1\(t\)=\\log t\. \\\] After normalization, $U=F\_q\(v\)$ and $M\_q\[v\]=vD^2v-qDv\\otimes Dv$ satisfies $σ\_k\(M\_q\[v\]\)=0$\. A unified contact-point calculation gives the sharp bound $\|Dv\|\\le b$;a boundary viscosity contact then yields $H\_k\\ge qbH\_\{k-1\}$\. The integral mechanism that closes the argument changes at the critical exponent: Rellich--Pohozaev identities are used for $q&gt;1$, a scale-invariant Wronskian--Newton current for $q=1$, and a renormalized Newton--Jacobi mass for $q&lt;1$\. When $q\\leq 1,$ we also obtain a global strict k-convex defining function by Minkowski gauge, this help us extend the exterior construction from convex domains to strictly star-shaped domains\.
