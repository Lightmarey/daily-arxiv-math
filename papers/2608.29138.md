**证明逻辑/大纲**

1. **主张：** Proposition 3\.1 构造 $u=W\+v$ 形式的广义弱解。
   - **路线：** 第3节先选 Poiseuille 基流，再对扰动使用截断、弱紧性和压力消元取得极限解。
2. **主张：** Proposition 3\.2 给出扰动 v 的远场点态衰减。
   - **路线：** 结合局部 Stokes 正则性与轴向加权能量估计，逐层控制速度和压力。
3. **主张：** Proposition 4\.1 证明小外力下唯一性。
   - **路线：** 对差方程 $w=v-U$ 进行能量积分，小 Poiseuille 梯度使对流项可吸收，推出 $w=0$。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we investigate the uniqueness and asymptotic behavior of the stationary Navier-Stokes equations in a slab domain with no-slip boundary conditions\. Specifically, under the given external force and the small Poiseuille flow assumptions, we prove that there is an H1 solution, and obtain the pointwise decay around the Poiseuille flow at far field\. Furthermore, if the force is small, the solution is shown to be unique\. The key point of the proof is the estimate of the Dirichlet integral of the solutions in the truncated domains and the Stokes regularity estimates\.
