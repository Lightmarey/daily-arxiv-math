**完成的工作**

论文研究带 Helfrich 弯曲能与曲率耦合反应扩散的几何 mechanochemical vesicle 模型，重点是平面闭曲线并辅以二维球面的展望。作者推导曲线形状、形态变量和约束乘子的耦合演化，先用线性化谱和对称性识别从圆到波数 m 分支的 pitchfork/transcritical bifurcation，再以 pde2path 的 arclength continuation 跟踪稳态、相对稳态和 Hopf 产生的周期轨道；DNS 显示 breathing/moving vesicle、漂移、触发波和自交失效。全文以数值延拓和直接模拟为主，没有独立的 theorem/lemma proof chain。

**使用技术**

- Helfrich geometric flow
- reaction–diffusion coupling
- equivariant bifurcation analysis
- arclength continuation
- pde2path/DNS

**可能的突破**

展示形态变量反馈如何把平面 vesicle 的稳态分支推进为呼吸与移动周期轨道，并给出可复现的数值延拓框架来追踪对称破缺和 Hopf 分岔。

**限制与不确定性**

结论主要来自离散化、数值延拓和 DNS，周期轨道稳定性及自交后的模型失效没有被解析证明；参数区间、网格质量和对称性选择影响分支图，三维曲面只作展望，不能把数值分支当作一般存在定理。

**原始英文摘要**

We consider a geometric mechanochemical model of vesicles which couples the Helfrich flow for the shape of a lipid bilayer vesicle membrane $X$ with a reaction-diffusion equations for a single \`\`morphogen'' $φ$ on $X$\. The Helfrich flow is the $L^2$ gradient flow of the elastic bending energy $E\(X\)=\\int\_X \(H-c\_0\)^2 dS$ of $X$, typically supplemented by area or volume constraints, or both\. The morphogen $φ$ adsorbs/desorbs at places of high/low mean curvature $H$, i\.e\., the kinetics of $φ$ depend on $H$, and conversely $φ$ modifies the spontaneous curvature $c\_0$ on $X$\. The flow is no longer gradient, and hence allows for more complicated dynamics, including time periodic orbits, e\.g\., \`\`breathing and moving'' vesicle shapes\. We show how to compute bifurcation diagrams for such solution branches via numerical continuation and bifurcation methods\. We mostly focus on \`\`planar'' vesicles \(1D closed curves\) but also give an outlook on 3D vesicles \(2D closed membranes\)\.
